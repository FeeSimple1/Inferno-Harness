INFERNO - CALL TO ARMS REFERENCE
================================================================================
Source: Inferno Rules of Play (Living Rules 2023-04-10), Section 3.5, page 14.
        Cross-referenced with: Official Errata (10 April 2023), Playbook
        "Call to Arms" section (pp 8-10), Playbook "Wrath - Declaring War"
        (p 5), Arts of War card listing (Playbook p 64), Lords Reference,
        Scenario Reference.
 
When the Rules of Play or Errata conflict with any other source, the Rules of
Play and Errata take precedence (per user instruction).
 
 
================================================================================
OVERVIEW
================================================================================
 
Call to Arms is an optional escalated Levy sequence that follows the regular
Muster segment. Either or both sides may declare; if any side declares, BOTH
sides follow the procedure once per Levy (Guelphs first, then Ghibellines).
Conduct at most one Call to Arms by the two sides per Levy.
 
The four (optional) steps for each side in order:
  [a] 3.5.1 Gather             - Free March of Lords toward Leading City
  [b] 3.5.2 Commander to Arms  - Disband and/or Muster the Commander
  [c] 3.5.3 Comune             - Bring out the Leading City's communal army
  [d] 3.5.4 Allies             - Extra Lordship OR automatic Muster of one Lord
 
Each step is voluntary and may be skipped.
 
SCENARIO EXCLUSIONS:
  - Scenario A (Dolenti Note):       Skip all Call to Arms (Preamble rule).
  - Scenario E (Lasciate Ogne Sper.): Skip all Call to Arms (Exhaustion rule).
  - Other scenarios may have their own restrictions (see SCENARIO RULES below).
 
 
================================================================================
WHEN CALL TO ARMS HAPPENS IN THE SEQUENCE OF PLAY
================================================================================
 
LEVY PHASE (3.0):
  3.1 Arts of War   (Capabilities or Events drawn)
  3.2 Pay
  3.3 Disband
  3.4 Muster        (regular Lordship-driven Muster)
  3.5 Call to Arms  <-- HERE, after both sides finish 3.4 Muster
  -> proceed to Campaign (4.0)
 
Per Playbook: "After the Ghibellines complete their Muster segment (3.4),
check whether anyone can trigger Call to Arms, by VP score or cards."
 
 
================================================================================
TRIGGER CONDITIONS - WHO MAY DECLARE
================================================================================
 
A side may declare Call to Arms if ANY of the following is true at this point
in the sequence of play:
 
  1. It has the lower victory score by at least four VP (2.2.5, 5.1), OR
  2. It drew a War Event that Levy (3.1.3), OR
  3. It plays the Treasurers Event (F23, Guelphs only) and pays 2 Coin.
 
NOTES ON TRIGGERS:
  - It does not matter who declares or how many times. If ANY Call to Arms
    occurs that Turn, each side follows the procedure once, Guelphs then
    Ghibellines.
 
  - War Events trigger eligibility for the drawing side regardless of whether
    the rest of the War Event text applied (i.e., the conditional shifts on
    the card need not have happened).
 
  - Other effects of War Events (shifts, etc.) occur EARLIER during 3.1.3
    Draw Events, independently of Call to Arms.
 
  - The order of declaring matters only when the Ghibellines are eligible and
    the Guelphs are Holding F23 Treasurers. In that case, the Guelph player
    may wait until the Ghibellines have decided whether to declare before
    committing the Treasurers card and 2 Coin.
 
WAR EVENTS BY SIDE (2 per side, 1 of 26 cards each):
  Guelph:     F25 Val d'Orcia, F26 Montalcino
  Ghibelline: S25 Maremma, S26 Montepulciano
 
GUELPH-ONLY OPTIONAL TRIGGER:
  F23 Treasurers (Hold Event):
    "Play to pay 2 Coin to declare Call to Arms OR to add 1 Treachery."
    - Two Coin must be removable from any Guelph Lord(s), who may be
      Besieged.
    - May be played during the Call to Arms segment, including after the
      Ghibellines have declined to declare.
    - Alternatively, the 2 Coin may be paid at any time to add a set-aside
      Guelph Treachery card to the Guelph Command deck (not a Call to Arms
      trigger in that case).
 
 
================================================================================
PROCEDURE - GUELPHS THEN GHIBELLINES
================================================================================
 
PLAYER ORDER: If Call to Arms occurs, Guelphs perform any or all of steps
3.5.1-3.5.4, then Ghibellines do so. Each step is independently optional.
 
 
================================================================================
STEP 3.5.1 - GATHER (Free March)
================================================================================
 
EFFECT: Each Unbesieged Lord of the side anywhere on the map may immediately
        March (4.3) using his Command rating (modified as applicable).
 
PARTICULARS:
  - Commanders may March with other Lords (4.3.1) as in regular Campaign
    March.
  - Lords are NOT marked as Moved. They do NOT Feed (4.8) for this March.
    (Per Playbook: "The community is supporting the war.")
  - No Provender or Loot is expended for this Gather March.
 
RESTRICTIONS:
  - A Gathering March may NOT at any point enter a Locale containing any
    Unbesieged or Unbypassed Enemy Lord, OR any un-Ruined Enemy Stronghold.
    (Enemy that is already Bypassed or Besieged does NOT block the March.)
  - Each Lord who Marches MUST end up closer to his side's Leading City
    (Firenze for Guelphs, Siena for Ghibellines). The Lord does not have to
    arrive there, but every Marching Lord must net closer.
  - Besieged Lords may NOT March (they're stuck per 4.3).
 
PLAYBOOK EXAMPLE NOTES:
  - A Lord already inside his Leading City cannot March (cannot get closer).
  - A Lord adjacent to the Leading City where the only entry is blocked by an
    Enemy presence cannot March (Gather restriction).
  - Intermediate stops are allowed only if the END Locale is closer to the
    Leading City than the starting Locale.
 
 
================================================================================
STEP 3.5.2 - COMMANDER TO ARMS
================================================================================
 
The "Commander" is the Podestà of the Leading City:
  - Guelph Commander:     Firenze Podestà     (at Firenze)
  - Ghibelline Commander: Siena Podestà       (at Siena)
 
OPTION A - DISBAND (optional first):
  If the Commander (with his Comune, if Mustered) is currently on the map,
  the player may Disband him as if At Service Limit (3.3.2). This:
    - Returns the Commander's cylinder to the Calendar.
    - Removes any added Vassal Forces, Assets, and Capability cards from his
      mat (and his Comune mat if Mustered) - significant cost.
    - The Comune is removed with the Commander (1.5.1, 1.6).
    - Triggers Disband effects per 3.3.2 (with Errata: 3.3.2 line 3 deletes
      "but trigger no Revolt or Treachery"; the Errata-corrected text governs.)
 
OPTION B - MUSTER (optional, follows Disband if used):
  Muster the Commander INSIDE his Leading City, per the usual rules (3.4.1),
  EXCEPT his cylinder may be in ANY Calendar box (not only at or left of the
  current Levy marker).
 
  CONSTRAINTS still in force:
    - The Leading City must NOT be Enemy aligned (no Muster if Firenze marked
      Ghibelline, or Siena marked Guelph).
    - Urban Army exception (3.4.1) applies: since the Leading City is the
      Commander's Main Seat, he may Muster there even if Besieged.
    - If Besieged, there must be room inside the Stronghold for his cylinder
      per 4.3.4 WITHDRAW (City Size limits cylinder count; Firenze and Siena
      are both Size 3).
    - The Fealty roll is required per 3.4.1 (no automatic success here -
      that's the Allies step).
 
PLAY NOTE: The Disband-then-Muster combo is the standard way to bring the
Commander home to the Leading City when he has wandered. The choice is a
tradeoff: lose your Commander's Vassals/Assets/Capabilities versus gain a
clean position inside the Leading City to enable step 3.5.3.
 
 
================================================================================
STEP 3.5.3 - COMUNE
================================================================================
 
REQUIREMENT: This step is available only if the side's Commander is now on
the map INSIDE his Leading City. This includes if he is Besieged or Bypassed
there. (Whether he arrived by March, by Muster in step 3.5.2, or by being
already there at the start of Call to Arms, doesn't matter.)
 
THIS IS THE ONLY WAY A COMUNE EVER ENTERS PLAY.
 
PROCEDURE (all voluntary; if the side chooses to Muster the Comune):
  1. If the Comune is not already Mustered, set out its mat with the Assets
     and Special Vassals it shows. Stack that side's Comune cylinder beneath
     its Commander cylinder. (If Besieged, the Comune cylinder must fit per
     4.3.4 WITHDRAW.)
  2. Muster Special Vassals and units on the Comune mat. If the Comune is in
     play, it MUST end up with at least:
       a. Its Carroccio (unless Captured), AND
       b. One Sestiere (Firenze) or one Terzo (Siena).
  3. Up to all remaining Unmustered Sestieri/Terzi may Muster. During Call
     to Arms, Muster Sestieri/Terzi AS DESIRED, WITHOUT ROLLING.
  4. The side MAY then reassign "This Lord" Capability cards between the
     Commander mat and the Comune mat, provided neither mat ends up with more
     than two such cards (3.4.4).
 
KEY DETAILS:
  - Mustering the Comune is voluntary. You don't have to do it even if your
    Commander is at the Leading City.
  - You don't have to Muster ALL Special Vassals, but you must include the
    Carroccio plus at least one Sestiere/Terzo if you bring the Comune out
    at all.
  - The Comune adds a SECOND mat (Forces, Assets, Vassals, cards) and a
    second cylinder stacked under the Commander.
  - Outside of Siege and combat (e.g., March, Feed), Commander+Comune act as
    a single Lord.
  - In Siege/Withdrawal/Forage counting (4.3.4, 4.5.1, 4.7.1), they count as
    TWO Lords for Stronghold Size purposes.
  - In Battle and Storm, they Array, fight, and Rout as TWO SEPARATE Lords
    (4.4.1-.4, 4.5.2). Event/Capability text referring to "Firenze" or
    "Siena" affects BOTH mats separately. "This Lord" Capabilities apply
    only to the mat they're tucked under.
  - The Comune remains with the Commander until: he Disbands, he is removed
    by combat, or the Comune loses all its Forces (1.6, 3.3, 4.4.5).
 
LATER MUSTER (outside Call to Arms) - 3.4.2 SESTIERI/TERZI:
  Once the Comune mat is in play with Ready Sestieri/Terzi remaining, a
  Commander INSIDE (not Bypassing or Besieging) his Leading City may attempt
  to Muster them via regular Levy actions. For each Levy action, select one
  Sestiere/Terzo and roll a die: roll <= the die shown on its Service marker
  Musters it; higher roll wastes the action. (Inside Call to Arms, no roll
  is needed.)
 
CARROCCIO SPECIAL NOTES (per 3.4.2 / 3.5.3 / 4.4.3 / 5.1):
  - Musters ONLY during Call to Arms (cannot be Mustered later, including
    via the Sestieri/Terzi 3.4.2 Levy action).
  - Ignores the Advanced Vassal Service rule limits (3.4.2).
  - If Retreated INTACT (not Lost/Captured) after a Battle, Lords on that
    side shift Service only 1 box left rather than the usual 2 (per 4.4.3
    EXCEPTION).
  - If taken as Spoils (Captured) - the marker, not its Forces - the OTHER
    side permanently captures it and gains 2 VP (5.1).
  - Cannot be Bribed (4.7.6 - Special Vassals immune).
 
 
================================================================================
STEP 3.5.4 - ALLIES
================================================================================
 
CHOOSE ONE (OR DO NEITHER):
 
OPTION 1 - Extra Muster Segment with One Lord:
  Conduct an extra Muster segment with any ONE Lord of that side who is able
  to do so per 3.4. He uses Levy actions equal to his Lordship rating, by
  the usual rules:
    - He must have begun this segment at a Friendly Locale and Unbesieged
      (Urban Army Podestà exception still applies: Besieged at Main Seat OK).
    - He may use Levy actions to: Levy Other Lords (Fealty roll), Levy
      Vassals, Levy Transport, or Levy Capabilities.
    - A Commander chosen here may Levy cards to his Comune mat as well.
 
OPTION 2 - Auto-Muster One Lord (NOT the Commander):
  Muster any ONE Lord OTHER than the Commander automatically:
    - No Fealty roll required.
    - No Levy action required.
    - Otherwise per 3.4.1 (including Urban Army exception for a Podestà at
      his Main Seat under Siege).
    - The Lord must still be Ready on the Calendar (cylinder at or left of
      the Levy marker), and have a Seat that is free, not Enemy-aligned,
      and not Ruined-and-occupied by Enemy.
    - Exile Army: A Podestà whose Main Seat is Enemy-aligned still requires
      a Fealty roll under regular Muster - BUT this option says "without a
      Fealty roll", which auto-succeeds. (The auto-Muster bypasses both the
      roll and the Levy action; the Lord must still find a free Seat.)
 
NOTE: A newly-Mustered Lord arriving in step 3.5.4 may NOT immediately Levy
anything else this Levy (per 3.4 REQUIREMENTS: a Lord just arriving on the
map cannot take Levy actions in the same Muster segment that brought him).

F25. VAL D'ORCIA - War (Guelph)
================================================================================
Trigger text:  If a Castle marked Guelph, shift Colle cylinder or Service 3
               Calendar boxes. Guelphs may declare Call to Arms.
 
Implementation (at moment of drawing in 3.1.3):
  - If any ORIGINALLY GHIBELLINE Castle (not Town or City) has Guelph 1VP
    markers on it, shift Colle's cylinder LEFT or his Service marker RIGHT
    by 3 boxes (player's choice; if Colle is on the Calendar, shift cylinder
    left; if Mustered, shift Service right).
  - The Capability on this card is NOT available this Levy.
  - After Muster (3.4), regardless of whether the conditional shift applied,
    Guelphs may declare Call to Arms (3.5) at the Call to Arms segment.

  - After Muster, regardless of whether the conditional shift applied,

F23. TREASURERS (Guelph) - Optional Trigger
================================================================================
Trigger text:  Hold: Play to pay 2 Coin to declare Call to Arms OR to add 1
               Treachery.
 
Implementation:
  - REQUIRES: Guelphs hold this Event (drawn but kept face-down) and can
    remove 2 Coin total from any Guelph Lord(s), who may be Besieged.
  - OPTION A: Play during the Call to Arms segment to trigger Call to Arms
    for the Guelphs (including AFTER the Ghibellines have declined to
    declare, which lets Guelphs see Ghibelline decision first).
  - OPTION B: Play at any time to pay the 2 Coin and add a set-aside Guelph
    Treachery card to the Guelph Command deck. (This option does NOT trigger
    Call to Arms.)

RELATED CAPABILITIES THAT INTERACT WITH CALL TO ARMS

F22. TAU COMPANY (Guelph Capability)
  Effect: Firenze and Lucca have Lordship +1 and may Muster Altopascio for
          0 actions.
  Call to Arms relevance: The +1 Lordship applies to BOTH the regular Muster
  (3.4) AND the Allies step of Call to Arms (3.5.4 Option 1), every Muster
  and Call to Arms while Tau Company is in effect.
  Errata: The sentence beginning "A Comune mat..." is DELETED from the
          Tips (per Page 46 errata).
 
S15. WAR LOANS (Ghibelline Event)
  Effect: Shift Siena, Provenzano, Giordano, OR Astimberg cylinder or Service
          2 boxes, OR this Levy give one of them Lordship +2.
  Call to Arms relevance: The +2 Lordship option applies to BOTH the regular
  Muster (3.4) AND the Allies step of Call to Arms (3.5.4 Option 1) this
  Levy.
 
S10. A BETTER PAID DEATH (Ghibelline Event)
  Effect: Shift 2 non-Podestà Ghibelline Lords by 1 box each, OR this Levy
          give 1 of them Lordship +2.
  Call to Arms relevance: As S15 - the +2 Lordship option applies to both
  regular Muster and the Allies step (3.5.4 Option 1) this Levy. No Podestà
  may receive the Lordship boost.
 
F21. DISTRINGITORES (Guelph Capability) and similar Battle-time Capabilities:
  Mentioned in the Playbook "Wrath" section as useful when preparing for
  field Battle, which Call to Arms often precedes.
 
KEY VASSAL/SPECIAL VASSAL INTERACTIONS:
  - Carroccio (both sides): Muster ONLY via Call to Arms step 3.5.3.
    Worth 2 VP to opponent if Captured. Mitigates Service loss if Retreated
    intact (4.4.3 EXCEPTION).
  - Sestieri/Terzi: Auto-Muster (no roll) only via Call to Arms step 3.5.3.
    Outside Call to Arms, they may be Mustered one at a time via Levy
    actions with a die roll (3.4.2 SESTIERI/TERZI).
  - Altopascio (Tau Company set-aside): Not a Call to Arms mechanism, but
    Tau Company's Lordship +1 carries through to the Allies step.

SCENARIO-SPECIFIC CALL TO ARMS RULES

   Preamble special rule: Skip all Call to Arms (3.5). The 3.5 rule section
   is entirely ignored.

   Escalation special rule: In the first Levy, EITHER side may trigger Call
   to Arms as if it had drawn a WAR Event (3.5). After the first Levy,
   standard 3.5 trigger rules apply.

   Exhaustion special rule: Skip all Call to Arms (3.5). The 3.5 rule
   section is entirely ignored.

   No scenario-specific Call to Arms rule. Standard 3.5 applies. Other
   special rules (Night March, Exhaustion-as-game-length-roll) do not
   affect Call to Arms.
 
 
================================================================================
STRATEGY NOTES (Per Playbook "Wrath - Declaring War and the Call to Arms")
================================================================================
 
Both players are likely to face the opportunity to declare Call to Arms at
some point. Declaring gives:
  - Automatic Muster of your Leading City's Podestà (Commander)
  - Mustering the Comune (Carroccio + Sestieri/Terzi as desired)
  - Free March of any of your Lords toward your Leading City
  - An additional Muster segment with one Lord (or auto-Muster a non-
    Commander Lord)
HOWEVER: Your opponent gets to do the same. Choose timing when the opponent
is LEAST able to make good use of the steps.
 
DEFENSIVE DECLARATION:
  - Useful when a large Enemy army is bearing down and your Commander is
    still Unmustered.
  - The Enemy is less likely to want to use the Call to Arms steps, so you
    use them more effectively.
 
OFFENSIVE DECLARATION:
  - Useful when you can March your Commander and Comune to an Enemy Town or
    City to conquer it before help arrives.
 
DESPERATE / BEHIND-IN-VICTORY DECLARATION:
  - When 4+ VP behind, you can declare regardless. You may use Commander
    and Comune fairly recklessly, since the catch-up potential is high.
 
LEADING-CITY ROOM CONSTRAINT:
  - Firenze (Size 3) under Siege: needs room for Commander + Comune
    cylinders. With the standard mats, three cylinders fit (Size 3); if
    others are already there, plan accordingly.
  - Same for Siena (Size 3).
 
CAPABILITIES TO COORDINATE WITH:
  - F22 Tau Company / S15 War Loans / S10 A Better Paid Death give Lordship
    bonuses that boost the Allies step (3.5.4 Option 1).
  - F23 Treasurers gives Guelphs the unique optional trigger via 2 Coin.
 
 
================================================================================
EXAMPLE - GUELPH CALL TO ARMS (Paraphrased from Playbook pp 9-10)
================================================================================
 
Setup state (continuing the Muster example):
  - Guelph Lord Guido Guerra: Besieged inside the Guelph Leading City area
    (cannot March)
  - Guelph Lord Arezzo Podestà: at Figline (adjacent to Firenze)
  - Guelph Lord Lucca Podestà: at Altopascio (just Mustered)
  - Guelph Commander (Firenze Podestà): Unmustered on the Calendar
  - Ghibelline Lords are Besieging Firenze
 
GUELPH CALL TO ARMS (proceeds first):
 
Step 3.5.1 Gather:
  - Guido Guerra: Cannot March (Besieged, AND already in the Leading City).
  - Arezzo Podestà: Cannot enter Firenze (Ghibelline Lords there); no other
    move would end closer to Firenze.
  - Lucca Podestà: Marches Altopascio -> San Miniato -> Empoli. (Cannot
    stop at San Miniato alone because that's not closer to Firenze along
    this path; Empoli IS closer.)
 
Step 3.5.2 Commander to Arms:
  - No Disband (Commander still on Calendar).
  - Muster Firenze Podestà AT Firenze, even though Firenze is Besieged
    (Urban Army exception applies at his Main Seat). Place cylinder at
    Firenze and Service marker on the Calendar 3 boxes ahead per his
    Service rating.
 
Step 3.5.3 Comune:
  - Commander is now at Firenze, with room (just barely - third cylinder
    inside the Size-3 City). The player Musters the Firenze Comune.
  - Place Comune cylinder under the Firenze Commander cylinder.
  - Set up the Firenze Comune mat with its Carroccio plus at least one
    Sestiere (required minimum).
  - Auto-Muster the remaining 5 Sestieri as desired (no rolling during
    Call to Arms).
 
Step 3.5.4 Allies:
  - Firenze (now as an Urban Army), Arezzo (at Figline, Friendly), or
    Lucca (at Empoli) could use Lordship to Levy. Guido Guerra cannot
    (Besieged, NOT a Podestà at Main Seat - actually Guido is not a
    Podestà at all, so no Urban Army exception).
  - The player picks one of those Lords (or auto-Musters a single Lord
    other than the Commander).
 
GHIBELLINE CALL TO ARMS (then proceeds second):
  - The Ghibelline player goes through 3.5.1-3.5.4 in turn, possibly
    Marching Lords south from the Siege of Firenze toward Siena,
    possibly Disbanding/Mustering Siena's Commander at Siena, etc.
 
 
================================================================================
COMMON QUESTIONS & EDGE CASES
================================================================================
 
Q: Can the Comune Muster outside Call to Arms?
A: No - the Comune ENTERS PLAY only via Call to Arms (3.5.3). However, once
   in play, additional Sestieri/Terzi may Muster later via regular Levy
   actions if the Commander is inside (not Besieging/Bypassing) his Leading
   City, with a die roll each (3.4.2 SESTIERI/TERZI).
 
Q: Can the Carroccio Muster outside Call to Arms?
A: No - the Carroccio Musters ONLY in Call to Arms (3.5.3).
 
Q: If my Commander Disbands in step 3.5.2, do I lose his "This Lord"
   Capabilities and Comune mat contents?
A: Yes - per standard Disband rules. Vassal Forces return to the pool,
   Capabilities are discarded, the Comune is removed with him. This is
   often the cost of re-positioning via 3.5.2.
 
Q: Can a Gather March end at a Ruined Enemy Stronghold?
A: Yes - the restriction is on un-Ruined Enemy Strongholds. A Ruined
   Stronghold's Allegiance still matters for Friendly/Enemy purposes but
   the restriction's wording excludes Ruined ones (the Lord may March
   through/into a Ruined Locale unless an Unbesieged/Unbypassed Enemy Lord
   is also there).
 
Q: Can the Allies Auto-Muster (Option 2) bring out a Commander?
A: No - per 3.5.4: "Muster any one Lord other than the Commander
   automatically". The Commander has his own dedicated step (3.5.2).
 
Q: Can a side declare Call to Arms via score AND another side also play
   F23 Treasurers?
A: Yes, but it doesn't matter how many declarations occur. Each side does
   the procedure once total (Guelphs first). The "first declare; then both
   sides proceed once each" structure stands.
 
Q: What if both sides drew a War Event the same Levy?
A: Either or both may declare; each side still proceeds through 3.5.1-3.5.4
   exactly once that Levy (Guelphs first, then Ghibellines). Note that
   shifts from the War Events themselves happen earlier during 3.1.3, not
   at this point.
 
Q: Can a Gathering Lord Bypass an Enemy along the way?
A: No - the restriction reads "may not at any point enter a Locale with
   any Unbesieged or Unbypassed Enemy Lord or un-Ruined Enemy Stronghold."
   Bypass requires being IN that Locale at end of action; the Gather March
   cannot enter that Locale at all unless the Enemy is ALREADY Bypassed or
   Besieged.
 
Q: Is the Comune a separate Lord for Capability Discard purposes?
A: No - per 4.0 CAPABILITY DISCARD: discard excess Capabilities compared to
   on-map Lords NOT including Comuni. The Commander and Comune together
   count as one Lord for that purpose.
