Inferno Forces

Horse forces

Ritter - two strikes in battle, one strike in a storm.  Ritter's armor protects them on 1-4.  Ritters and Cavalieri are the only units eligible for Knights' Qurater.

Cavalieri - one strike in battle or storm (this can be affected by the Feditori capability or army reserve card.  Cavalieri armor protects them on 1-3.  Ritters and Cavalieri are the only units eligible for Knights' Qurater.

Berrovieri - half strike in battle, half strike in storm as a defender, and a full strike for storm as attacker.  Berrovieri armor protects them on a 1-2.  Berrovieri generate double Provender and Loot when they ravage.

Light Horse - half strike in all cases.  Light horse armor protects them on 1.

Foot forces

Men-at-Arms - men at arms have half Crossbow archery (remember that this also allows them to choose their target), but only when they are the Garrison in a storm or when the Balestre Grosse card is in play.  In all other circumstances, they have one strike.  Men-at-Arms armor protects them on 1-3.

Armigieri - Armigieri have a full Crossbow archery (remember that this also allows them to choose their target), but only when they are the Garrison in a storm or when Balestrieri is in play.  The Palvasari card substantially changes their capabilities.  In all other circumstances, they have one strike.  Armigieri armor protects them on 1-2, or 1-4 if Palvasari is in play.

Militia - Militia have one non-Crossbow archery strike (this does NOT allow them to choose their target), if they are in in the Garrison or the Arcieri card is in play for them.  If Luceria is in play instead of Arcieri, then they have a strike and a half of archery.  In all other circumstances, they have one strike.  Militia armor protects them on 1.

Villici - Villici have a half strike in all circumstances.  They have no armor and are automatically eliminated if hit.

Inferno Strongholds

All strongholds are protected by walls on a 1-4 die roll.  All values below are in addition to those provided by any defending lords in the city.

Level 3 City - City garrisons have three men-at-arms (each with a half crossbow worth of strike in addition to their melee capabilities), three armigieri, each with a full crossbow worth of strike, and three cavalieri.  Cities roll three surrender/revolt dice.

Level 2 Town - Town garrisons have two men-at-arms (each with a half crossbow worth of strike in addition to their melee capabilities), two armigieri, each with a full crossbow worth of strike, and two cavalieri.  Towns roll two surrender/revolt dice.

Level 1 Castle - Castle garrisons have one man-at-arms (with a half crossbow worth of strike in addition to its melee capabilities), one militia (with a regular, non-crossbow archery strike), and one cavalieri.  Castles roll one surrender die.
