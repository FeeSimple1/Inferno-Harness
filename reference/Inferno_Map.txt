ANNOTATION KEY
--------------
For each Locale that is a Seat, two annotation types are added:
  - Lord Seat:   Lord(s) for whom this Locale is a Seat (1.5.1, 3.4.1)
  - Vassal Seat: Vassal(s) whose Vassal Seat is at this Locale (1.5.4, 3.4.2)
                 Format: Vassal Name (parent Lord)
  - Special:     Notes about Comune Special Vassals (Sestieri, Terzi, Carroccio)
                 which Muster at the Leading City but have NO Vassal Seats per
                 rule 3.4.2 EXCEPTION.

TERRITORIAL DIVISION (Green Dashed Line, Rules 1.3.1)
================================================================================

Ghibelline Locales (SW). Affects Ravage targeting, Friendly/Enemy status,
Muster, Withdrawal, Pay with Loot. In Scenario C only, Ghibellines may not
cross the line until Guelphs place a Siege or Ravage marker.

NOTES & OBSERVATIONS
================================================================================
 
CROSSROADS RULE (1.3.1): A path between two adjacent Locales that uses some
Road and some Track counts as Track. So when a Track joins a Road segment
mid-way, the original Road endpoints stay Road-connected to each other, but
new Track adjacencies are created from the Track Locale to both Road endpoints.
 
FAST CORRIDORS (all Road):
- Firenze - Figline - San Giovanni - Montevarchi - Arezzo (Guelph spine)
- Arezzo - Bibbiena - Poppi - Battifolle - L-B East (Guelph reinforcement)
- Pisa - Rosignano - Campiglia - Massa Marittima - Montemassi - Grosseto
  (Ghibelline coastal)
- Radicofani - San Quirico - Siena - Monteriggioni - Colle (north-south
  central, crosses the dashed line)
 
CHOKEPOINTS:
- Poggio Bonizio: only Track connections; sits on the Firenze-Siena friction
  axis. The Playbook example shows the Ghibelline army pausing here.
- Monte San Savino: 5-way junction in border zone; scenarios place Ravaged
  and Ruins here.
- The Arno Ports: Pisa-Pontedera-San Miniato-Empoli-Montelupo-Firenze; all
  Track between Ports despite the river, but Ports allow Pisa's Sail (4.7.3)
  and serve Supply Routes that ignore Ruins (4.6.1).
 
OUTPOST CONNECTIONS:
- Lombardia-Bologna West: Ponte a Sieve (T) only
- Lombardia-Bologna East: Battifolle (R) only - the fastest reinforcement
  route into Tuscany
- Orvieto: Cortona (T), Chiusi (T)
 
LEADING CITIES (Firenze and Siena) are NOT directly Road-linked. Movement
between them must transit Track, typically via Poggio Bonizio, San Gimignano,
or Colle - matching the historical friction of 1260 campaigns.
