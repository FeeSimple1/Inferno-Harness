================================================================================
INFERNO – ARTS OF WAR CARD REFERENCE
================================================================================
Source: Inferno Playbook (Background Book), Arts of War – Notes (pp. 33-55)
Errata applied: Official Errata 10 April 2023

Card structure: each card has an upper Event (fleeting, 3.1.3) and a lower
Capability (lasting, 3.4.4). Only one section is in effect at a time. When
card text contradicts the rules, card text takes precedence (1.9.1).

Bold-italic markers in card text:
  Hold        – keep drawn Event hidden for later use at holder's discretion.
  This Levy   – Event in effect to end of current Levy.
  This Campaign – Event in effect to end of current Campaign.
  This Lord   – Capability tied to a specific Lord's mat.

================================================================================
GUELPH EVENTS (top half of card)
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F1. AMBUSH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Hold: Play as Enemy Avoids Battle (4.3.4). Enemy chooses 1 Avoiding
       Lord to Battle or Withdraw instead.
Lords: Any Ghibelline.
Tips:  Play after one or a group of inactive Lords declare Avoid Battle
       (4.3.4) forces the owner to choose any one of those Lords (including
       Commander with Comune) to instead stay in the Locale and either Defend
       in Battle or Withdraw into a Friendly Stronghold there (if it has one).
       Other Avoiding Lords must depart.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F2. BETRAYALS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  For each Stronghold with any Guelph Siege markers, roll on Revolt
       table or add 1 Treachery.
Lords: Any Guelph (for Treachery).
Tips:  Count the number of Sieges underway by the side playing the card (each
       Besieged Enemy Stronghold counts as one). That side may choose to roll
       on its Revolt table a number of times (1.4.2) plus select and add a
       number of currently set-aside Treachery cards to its Command deck
       (1.4.3) in sum equal to that number of Besieged Strongholds. The
       player may see the outcome of a Revolt roll before deciding what to
       choose next.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F3. SURPRISE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Hold: Play upon Besieging a Stronghold where no other Lord (4.3.5).
       Place 2 Siege (not 1). Then Storm up to 3 Rounds, Walls -2.
Lords: Any Guelph.
Tips:  The Lord involved must be of the side playing the card and may not be
       a group (neither Commander with other Lords nor a Lieutenant, but may
       be a Commander with Comune). The Lord must have just Marched to an
       Enemy Stronghold Locale where there are neither Enemy nor Friendly
       Lords already. Place two Siege markers instead of the usual one
       (4.3.5). An immediate Storm for at least one Round is mandatory
       (4.5.2), costing no actions, and may continue to three Rounds even
       though there are only two Siege markers. Walls -2 means treat Walls
       1-4 as Walls 1-2 (or 1-3 if Walls +1 marker). Siegeworks of two
       markers will give the Attacker Walls 1-2 (4.5.2).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F4. SUDDEN CLASH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Hold: Play on a Guelph Lord in Battle. Round 1, that Lord's Horse
       Melee precedes all Archery and selects its targets.
Lords: Any Guelph.
Tips:  Use of the Event shifts Melee by Horse units on one mat (not a
       Commander's Podestà and Comune both) of the playing side in Round 1
       ahead of both sides' Archery in the Strike Initiative order (4.4.2).
       If both sides play Sudden Clash, the selected Defending Lord's Horse
       Melee is first, then affected Attacker's, then the rest. Also, that
       Lord's Horse Melee Round 1 selects targets as described under 4.4.2
       ASSIGN HITS TO UNITS.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F5. ROAD WORKS – Woodcutters clear the way
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  This Campaign Guelph Lords treat Track as Road and move Laden as if
       Unladen.
Lords: All Guelphs.
Tips:  Any Guelph this March can take up to two Provender per Cart Unladen,
       but not more than that. Also, the first such March Way costs 0
       actions even if Track (4.3.2-.3). Discard the card at Turn's end.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F6. HILLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Hold: May play Defending in Battle. Double Guelph Archery Hits, added
       Hits as Bowmen not Crossbow.
Lords: All Guelphs.
Tips:  Play Hills at the outset of Battle (4.4.1 Events), not in Storm and
       only if Defending. It increases the Hits from all of your side's
       Archery throughout that Battle. It does not affect Melee. Total all
       Hits against each target Lord, double that amount, then round. Any
       added Hits from doubling Crossbow Archery do not reduce Enemy Armor
       and never select their targets (4.4.2 ASSIGN HITS TO UNITS). Round
       any such odd or half Hits to count as Crossbows.
       EXAMPLE: Firenze with its Comune Flanking Strike a single Ghibelline
       Lord. The Podestà has 3x Militia and Arcieri, which would usually
       total three Archery Hits. The Comune has 5x Armigeri with Balestrieri
       and Palvesari, which usually would add five Crossbow Hits (-2 to
       Armor) that select targets. If Defending with Hills, the two Firenze
       mats Striking a single Ghibelline Lord would double the usual eight
       Archery Hits to a mighty 16 Hits, of which still only five Hits would
       count as Crossbows and thus select their targets and apply a -2 to
       Armor Protection rolls.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F7. GREEK FIRE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Hold: Play on Besieging Lord to reduce to 1 Siege marker, remove 1 of
       his units, and discard 1 of his "This Lord" Capabilities.
Lords: Any Ghibelline.
Tips:  Play at any time (even in the midst of Storm). Choose any one Enemy
       Lord outside any Besieged Stronghold (the Lord need not be Active).
       Select one unit and one "This Lord" Capability card (if any) at his
       mat(s): he removes that unit to the pool and discards that card.
       Remove any Siege markers there beyond one.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F8. SWAMP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Hold: May play Defending in non-Summer Battle. Ghibelline Horse does
       not Strike in Round 1.
Lords: All Ghibellines.
Tips:  Play at the outset of Battle (4.4.1 Events), not Storm, not on Attack,
       and not if the current Turn is in Summer. It prevents all Horse units
       of all Enemy Lords from Striking in the first Round. It does affect
       their ability to absorb Hits.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F9. GENOVA breaks Pisan blockade
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Shift Firenze, Lucca, and Colle cylinder or Service 1 Calendar box
       each or add 1 of their Treachery.
Lords: Firenze, Lucca, and Colle.
Tips:  The Guelph player chooses the direction of all shifts so would shift
       all these Friendly cylinders on the Calendar left and Service markers
       right. Instead, the player could select any one Treachery card that is
       currently set aside (not one already available or in the Plan) to add
       to the Guelph Command deck.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F10. CLOSED GATES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Remove 1 gold Ruins or, if none, remove 1 purple Ruins where
       Stronghold then eligible for Revolt (1.4.1); it Revolts (1.4.4).
Lords: None.
Tips:  Remove any one Ruins giving the other side ½VP. If there are none on
       the map, instead replace a Ruins benefitting your side with Allegiance
       markers equal to the Stronghold Value. Eligible means that there
       cannot be an Enemy Lord there or adjacent. [Errata: "or adjacent"
       added.]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F11. POGGIO BONIZIO answers Firenze's call
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Hold: Play if Poggio Bonizio Guelph to give a Lord Lordship +3 OR add
       1 Treachery.
Lords: Any Guelph.
Tips:  Play the Event only at any moment that Poggio Bonizio (near map
       center) is free of Ghibelline 1VP (gold) Allegiance markers. Play it
       during either Muster (3.4) or, if declared, Call to Arms Allies
       (3.5.4) to add three to the Lordship rating of any one Guelph Lord.
       Or play it to select any one set-aside Guelph Treachery card—not one
       then in the Plan—to add to the Command deck.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F12. CAMP ATTACK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Hold: My play in Battle. Round 1, take 2 Assets from each Enemy Lord
       there as Spoils, then remove 2 more from each.
Lords: All Ghibellines.
Tips:  Play Camp Attack at the outset of Battle (4.4.1 Events), not Storm.
       Take and remove Enemy Assets as specified on the card (including from
       both a Commander's Podestà and Comune as separate Lords) at any one
       time during Round 1, distributing taken Assets among Friendly Lords
       at the Battle (4.4.3) even if Conceding the Field. Spoils may not
       include Ships, but removed Assets may (representing other affairs
       diverting the Pisan navy).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F13. NO TIME FOR COWARDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Shift Firenze, Arezzo, and Lucca cylinders or Service 1 Calendar box
       each or add 1 of their Treachery.
Lords: Firenze, Arezzo, Lucca.
Tips:  The Guelph player chooses the direction of shifts (Friendly cylinders
       left and Service markers right) or instead selects any one set-aside
       Guelph Treachery card to add to the Command deck.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F14. PROVENZANO sent as ambassador
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  If Guelph Siege underway, shift Provenzano cylinder or Service 2
       Calendar boxes or add 1 Treachery.
Lords: Provenzano (for shift) or any Guelph (for Treachery).
Tips:  Implement this Event only if at least one Guelph (purple) Siege marker
       is somewhere on the map. The Guelph player chooses whether to shift
       Provenzano's cylinder right or Service marker left (if either is on
       the Calendar) or to add any one set-aside Treachery card to the
       Guelph Command deck.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F15. CASOLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Hold: Play for Firenze Treachery at a Castle to succeed for 0 Coin or
       to add Firenze Treachery.
Lords: Firenze.
Tips:  Play at the moment that Firenze's Podestà uses his Treachery card for
       Revolt (4.7.5) or Bribe (4.7.6) when the target is or is at a Castle
       for that Treachery to succeed automatically without committing or
       paying any Coin. For Revolt, the Castle must be eligible (1.4.1). Or
       play whenever Firenze's Treachery card (only) remains or has been set
       aside (neither in the Command deck nor in the Plan) to add it to the
       Guelph deck.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F16. A BLOODY RED STREAM – Desperate stand
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Hold: Play in Battle for first Guelph Lord who Routs instead to roll
       each unit's Protection now to recover from Rout.
Lords: Any Guelph.
Tips:  Play at the outset of Battle (4.4.1 Events). At the first moment
       (only) in that Battle that absorbing a Hit causes a Guelph Lord to
       Rout (4.4.2, possibly either a Commander's Podestà or Comune), pause
       and roll each of his Routed units' unmodified Protection as if
       checking for Loss (4.4.4)—success shifts those units back up into
       the Forces section of their mat; failure has no effect. If at least
       one Routed unit recovers, that Lord does not (yet) Rout. Then continue
       with any additional Hits and Battle steps.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F17. FOREIGN HELP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Shift Guido or Orvieto Service 2 boxes or, if desired, set aside 1
       Treachery from Guelph deck to shift their cylinders left to current
       Levy.
Lords: Guido Guerra or Orvieto (for shifts) and any Guelph (for Treachery).
Tips:  Shift Service right, or instead select any one Guelph Treachery card
       already acquired for and still in the Guelph Command deck (not from
       the Plan) to set aside again in order to shift one or both Guido
       Guerra's or Orvieto's cylinder into the current Turn's box from
       anywhere on the Calendar to the right of that box. If neither Service
       marker is on the Calendar, the Guelph player may but is not required
       to so set aside a Treachery card. If setting aside a Treachery card
       and only one of the two named Lords has a cylinder on the Calendar to
       the right of the current Turn, the player may move that cylinder only.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

       [Errata: last sentence beginning "The benefit..." deleted.]

F20. HEAT & FROST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Play before Feed in Summer or Winter for Wastage (4.9.5) now, twice

Lords: All Ghibellines and all Guelphs.
Tips:  Implement the Event only if Summer or Winter and immediately before

F21. PRIMO POPOLO – Guelph merchant class
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

F22. MANFREDI holds back
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Select a Lord with Ritter. Shift his cylinder or Service 1 Calendar
       box or remove 3 of his Assets.
       [Errata: "Ghibelline" deleted from "a Ghibelline Lord with Ritter".]
Lords: Any Ghibelline (who has or has obtained at least one Ritter).

================================================================================
GUELPH CAPABILITIES (bottom half of card)
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F1. STORES & WELL WATER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Guelph Seats each can Supply up to 4 Provender, even to a Lord
       Besieged there.
Lords: All Guelphs.
Tips:  With Stores & Well Water in effect, Supply can provide up to four
       Provender from each Seat used as a Source, rather than Provender
       equal to Stronghold Size, including from Ruined Seats and from
       Outposts. The Capability also allows a Lord to take Supply actions
       while Besieged at his own Seat, using that Seat as a Source. Supply
       otherwise still follows the usual rules, including the need for
       Transport (4.6). Stores & Well Water does not affect Supply using
       Ships and Ports.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F2. GUASTATORI – Sappers
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  This Lord's Siege adds 2 markers instead of 1. His Pass (4.7.7)
       replaces Enemy Siege there with Bypass.
Lords: Any Guelph.
Tips:  A Lord with Guastatori when first Besieging a Stronghold still places
       just a single Siege marker (4.3.5). With enough Lords present to add
       markers by the usual Siege rules (4.5.1), he would add two markers
       instead of one when taking a Siege action, so increasing markers from
       one to three or from two to four (maximum) on a single card. If
       Besieged, a Lord with Guastatori when activated by his Command actions
       or Treachery card could Pass (and do nothing else, 4.7.7) to remove
       all Enemy Siege markers and place Bypass on the Enemy Lords at his
       Locale. A Comune mat with this card gives the Commander the effects.
       Other Lords at a Siege than the Lord with Guastatori do not gain
       either effect.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F3. SIEGE TOWERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  This Lord Attacking in Storm from Round 2 on Strikes first with
       Archery and Melee.
Lords: Any Guelph.
Tips:  Any Lord with Siege Towers at a Storm Locale gains the benefit; it
       need not be the Lord taking the Storm action. A Commander's Podestà
       or Comune mat with this card gets the effect, not both. On Round 1,
       there is no effect. For Round 2 and all later Rounds, Initiative
       order is all this Lord's Strikes first, then continue in the normal
       order for Garrison and other Lords (4.4.2, 4.5.2 STRONGHOLD EFFECTS).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F4. TREBUCHETS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  This Lord Unrouted at Storm or Sally where 3-4 Siege reduces Enemy
       Siegeworks or Walls -1.
Lords: Any Guelph.
Tips:  Any Lord mat with this card participating in a Storm or Sally grants
       the effect to its entire side, so long as that mat (including a
       Commander's Podestà or Comune mat separately) has not Routed.
       EXAMPLES: Storming with Trebuchets reduces Walls 1-4 to Walls 1-3
       (4.5.2). Sallying with Trebuchets against three Siege markers reduces
       Siegeworks to Walls 1-2 (4.5.3).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F5. WAR ENGINEERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  This Lord's Siege adds marker to any size Stronghold. Besieged, he
       can use entire Command to reduce to 1 Siege there.
Lords: Any Guelph.
Tips:  A Lord with War Engineers can upon his Siege action add a marker
       regardless of Stronghold Size or how many Lords are present. "Entire
       Command" means this Lord when Besieged may devote all actions on his
       Command action card (not a Treachery card) to remove all but one
       Enemy Siege marker there. A Comune mat with this card gives the
       Commander the effect.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F6 & F7. FEDITORI – Vanguard knights
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Up to 4 of this Lord's Cavalieri in Battle Melee Strike x2 Rounds 1
       and 2.
Lords: Any Guelph.
Tips:  Feditori has no effect in Storm or from Round 3 of Battle on. A
       Commander's Podestà or Comune mat with this card gets the effect,
       not both mats. A mat may have only one Feditori card.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F8. BALESTRE GROSSE – Large crossbows
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  This Lord's Men-at-Arms in Storm have Crossbows x½, -2 to target's
       Armor, that select targets if Defending.
Lords: Any Guelph.
Tips:  Balestre Grosse has no effect in Battle and, in Storm, enables this
       Lord's Men-at-Arms Crossbows to select targets (4.4.2 ASSIGN HITS)
       only if Defending, not if Attacking. Crossbow units Strike with both
       Missile and Melee. Palvesari do not affect Balestre Grosse Men-at-Arms
       Crossbows because they only enhance Armigeri. A Commander's Podestà
       or Comune mat with this card gets the effect, not both mats (should
       a Comune obtain Turncoat Men-at-Arms).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F9-F11. BALESTRIERI – Crossbowmen
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Up to 3 of this Lord's Armigeri have Crossbows x1, -2 to target Armor,
       that select targets in Storm Defense.
Lords: Any Guelph.
Tips:  Balestrieri give up to three of this Lord's Armigeri Crossbow Archery
       in Battle or Storm, on Attack or Defense. Crossbow units Strike with
       both Missile and Melee. The Crossbowmen select targets (4.4.2 ASSIGN
       HITS) only if Defending in Storm, not if in Battle or if Attacking
       in Storm. See F13-F15/S13-S15 Palvesari, however, which if with the
       same Lord enable all his Armigeri Crossbows always to select targets.
       A Commander's Podestà or Comune mat with this card gets the effect,
       not both mats. A mat may have only one Balestrieri card.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F12. ARMY RESERVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Firenze, Arezzo, and Lucca Cavalieri in Battle Melee Strike x2 from
       Round 3 on.
Lords: Firenze, Arezzo, and Lucca.
Tips:  Firenze Podestà, Firenze Comune, Arezzo, and Lucca, whichever are
       involved in the Battle, each gain the effect. Army Reserve has no
       effect in Storm or during Rounds 1 or 2 of Battle.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F13-F15. PALVESARI – Shieldmen
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  This Lord's Armigeri have Armor 1-4, until 3 Rout. Their Crossbows
       always select targets.
Lords: Any Guelph.
Tips:  Palvesari give the Armor benefit (an increase from Armor 1-2 to Armor
       1-4) to all that Lord mat's Armigeri units. At the moment that a
       third Armigeri unit on that mat becomes Routed, the Armor benefit is
       cancelled for all Armigeri remaining among that mat's Forces. In
       addition, Palvesari enables all Armigeri Crossbows (using Balestrieri
       at the same mat) to select their targets (4.4.2 ASSIGN HITS)
       regardless of Storm or Battle, Attack or Defense, even after three
       Armigeri rout. A Commander's Podestà or Comune mat with this card
       gets the effect, not both. A mat may have only one Palvesari card.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F16 & F17. ARCIERI – Bowmen
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  This Lord's Militia have Archery x1.
Lords: Any Guelph.
Tips:  Bowmen Militia units Strike with both Missile and Melee. A
       Commander's Podestà or Comune mat with this card gets the effect (if
       they have Militia), not both mats. A mat may have only one Arcieri
       card.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F18. LA CAVALLATA – Long-range raiders
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  This Lord may pay 1 Prov to use entire Command to Ravage adjacent
       Locale, not at Unbesieged Enemy Lord.
Lords: Any Guelph.
Tips:  This Lord must have or Share (1.5.2) one Provender and use all
       actions on his Command action card (not a Treachery card) to use this
       Capability. Expend the Provender and Ravage an adjacent Locale, which
       for a Ghibelline Lord may be an Unravaged Guelph Outpost. The target
       Locale may not have an Unbesieged Enemy Lord, even if Bypassed.
       Otherwise follow Ravage rules—place a Ravaged marker and gain Loot
       or Provender normally (4.7.2). A Comune mat with this card gives the
       Commander the effect.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F19. GUALDANA – Horse marauders
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  This Lord treats any Horse unit as Berrovieri for Ravage.
Lords: Any Guelph.
Tips:  A Lord with Gualdana and any Horse unit(s) on his mat(s) can Ravage
       for double Provender and Loot (4.7.2) even if none of his units are
       Berrovieri. A Comune mat with this card gives the Commander the
       effect, as long as either mat has at least one Horse unit.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F20. MASNADIERI – Looters
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  This Lord may Ravage for 0 actions, taking no Loot.
Lords: Any Guelph.
Tips:  A Lord with Masnadieri uses zero actions to Ravage instead of one at
       a Castle or two at a Town or City. The Lord must still be active on
       his Command actions card and otherwise follow Ravage rules (4.7.2).
       Masnadieri can combine in effect with Gualdana for double Loot and
       Provender but does not aid Ravage adjacent with La Cavallata because
       La Cavallata requires an entire Command card rather than a set
       number of actions. A Comune mat with Masnadieri gives that Commander
       the Capability's effect.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F21. DISTRINGITORES – Officers dress ranks
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Each Round after Archery, a Guelph Podestà may roll 2 Routed units'
       Armor for them to recover.
Lords: Firenze, Arezzo, Lucca, and Colle.
Tips:  The owning side each Battle or Storm Round after all Archery and
       before all Melee that Round selects up to any two Routed units with
       Armor on that side (so, not Light Horse or Militia). Roll against
       modified Armor as applicable; Armigeri with Palvesari would roll
       Armor 1-4, for example. If successful, slide recovered units back up
       into the mat's Forces section. "A Guelph Podestà" means a single
       Podestà Lord in Battle or Storm each Round, so may be a Commander's
       Podestà or Comune mat but not both in the same Round. Only Armored
       units may roll, not Unarmored Light Horse or Militia.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F22. TAU COMPANY – Chivalric order
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Firenze and Lucca have Lordship +1 and may Muster Altopascio for 0

       [Clarification: Even though the card has no Lords symbol, any
       Ghibelline Lord can Levy this Capability per 3.4.3.]

S22. MANFREDI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Ghibelline Lords except Pisa start at any Lord Seat (not Vassal Seat)
       that is Friendly Command +1.
Lords: All Ghibellines except Pisa.
Tips:  "Command +1" means a Lord with Command Rating "2" instead could take
       three Command actions on his card, for example (1.5.3, 4.2.1). The
       added Command applies for that Lord's entire Command card, regardless
       of whether the Lord ends up still at a Friendly Lord Seat.

S23. TAGLIA – Mercenary army
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Non-Podestà Ghibelline Lords may Muster 1 Unmustered Vassal of
       another; when they do, they add 2x Carts.
Lords: Giordano, Astimberg, Provenzano, and Santa Fiora.
Tips:  Each of Giordano, Astimberg, Provenzano, and Santa Fiora may Muster
       one Vassal to their own Forces, along with two Carts each, otherwise
       following normal Vassal Muster rules (3.4.2). The Vassals may be
       those of any other of those four Lords. The borrowed Vassals may not
       currently be Mustered, including as Turncoats (4.7.6). Borrowed
       Vassals Disband normally, such as when the host Lord Disbands (3.3).

S24. ASTROLOGERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Each Ghibelline Lord on his first Command card each Campaign on die
       roll of 1-2 is Command +1.
Lords: All Ghibellines.
Tips:  See F24 Astrologers. On each revealed Command card, check whether it
       is the first card for that Lord that Campaign (including any
       Treachery cards). If it is (and is an actions, not Treachery card),
       roll one die: on 1-2, add one to that Lord's Command Rating for that
       card.

S25. REINFORCED WALLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Ghibelline Tax may instead mark Seat used Walls +1 if not already
       marked. Remove if Ruins.
Lords: All Ghibellines.
Tips:  See F25 Reinforced Walls. Requires a Tax action by usual rules
       (4.7.4) except Lord receives no Coin; instead place an available
       "Walls +1" marker in his side's color at his Locale (his Stronghold
       Seat, 1.3.1 WALLS +1). Seat may not be Ruins, an Outpost, or already
       marked Walls +1. Walls +1 markers are a limit on play; no effect if
       side already has four such markers on the map. S23 Economic
       Sanctions blocks Guelph use of Reinforced Walls (the latter requires
       Tax Commands).

S26. COSTRUTTORI – Master stonecutters
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Text:  Ghibelline Lords for a full Command card, 1 Coin, and 3 Prov remove
       Ruins from their Locale.
Lords: All Ghibellines.
Tips:  See F26 Costruttori. Lord must have or Share (1.5.2) one Coin and
       three Provender and devote all actions on his Command actions card
       (not Treachery) to remove any Ruins marker at his current Locale.
       Adjust Victory.
