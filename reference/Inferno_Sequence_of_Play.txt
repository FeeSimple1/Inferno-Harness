INFERNO - SEQUENCE OF PLAY REFERENCE (Turn Flow Checklist)

Source: Inferno Rules of Play (Living Rules 2023-04-10), primarily Sections

================================================================================
GAME STRUCTURE OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Each scenario is a series of 60-Day TURNS. Each Turn has two phases:

  LEVY (3.0)      - Prepare armies. Resolved in order, both sides.
  CAMPAIGN (4.0)  - Activate Lords by alternating Command cards until
                    both Plan stacks are exhausted.

After Campaign, perform End Campaign steps (4.9). If not the final Turn,
advance the Campaign marker to the next 60-Days' Levy and repeat.

Player order: Guelphs first in each step, Ghibellines second, unless noted
(2.2.4). Exceptions: alternating Command card play (4.2), and many Battle/
Siege/Storm sub-steps that depend on who is Active/Attacking/etc.

================================================================================
LEVY PHASE (3.0) - STEP-BY-STEP CHECKLIST
================================================================================

================================================================================
STEP 3.1 - ARTS OF WAR
================================================================================

  [ ] 3.1.1 SHUFFLE each side's unused Arts of War cards into an Event
            draw deck. Exclude Held Events and Capabilities in play.

  IF this is the scenario's FIRST Levy:
    [ ] 3.1.2 DRAW CAPABILITIES - Guelphs then Ghibellines each draw 2
              random Arts of War cards. Deploy each as a Capability
              (lower half).
              - Side-wide Capability: tuck at side's map edge.
              - "This Lord" Capability: tuck at a Mustered Lord's mat
                (max 2 per mat).
              - Discard any "This Lord" Capability that cannot be
                assigned to a Mustered Lord.

  IF this is the SECOND or any later Levy:
    [ ] 3.1.3 DRAW EVENTS - Guelphs then Ghibellines each draw 2 cards
              and implement the Event (upper half) in draw order.
              - "Hold" Events: keep hidden for later use at holder's
                discretion. Tuck under a Lord's mat top edge if specified.
              - "This Levy" / "This Campaign" Events: keep out until end
                of current Levy / Campaign.
              - Other Events resolve immediately and return to deck.
              - "War" Events: enable that side to declare Call to Arms
                later this Levy (3.5).
              - GREED (3.1.4): players may not discard cards except as
                rules permit.

================================================================================
STEP 3.2 - PAY
================================================================================

  Guelphs then Ghibellines. Each side may have its Lords expend Coin
  and/or Loot to shift Service markers right on the Calendar.

  [ ] 3.2.1 PAY WITH COIN
        - 1 Coin = 1 Service box shift (right) for the paying Lord OR
          another Lord at the same Locale.
        - PODESTA EXCEPTION: 2 Coin per box shift for a Podesta.

  [ ] 3.2.2 PAY WITH LOOT
        - Lord must be at a Friendly Locale FREE OF SIEGE (may be
          Bypassed).
        - 1 Loot = 1 Coin equivalent. 2 Loot/Coin per box for Podesta.

  NOTE: Coin and Loot are removed only to actually shift Service markers
  at this step.

================================================================================
STEP 3.3 - DISBAND
================================================================================

  Guelphs then Ghibellines.

  [ ] 3.3.1 BEYOND SERVICE LIMIT
        Lords whose Service marker is LEFT of the current Levy or
        Campaign box are removed.
        - Regular Lord: cylinder removed permanently.
        - Podesta: cylinder placed 3 boxes ahead of current Turn (= his
          Service Rating), may Muster again.
        - Set aside Service markers, mats, Forces (return to pool),
          Assets, Vassal markers, Comune if any.
        - Return "This Lord" Capabilities to that side's Arts of War deck.
        - REVOLT & TREACHERY: Enemy side rolls on Revolt table and
          adds Treachery cards (once for regular Lord, 3x for Podesta;
          equal to Service Rating). Comune Disband adds no Revolt/
          Treachery.

  [ ] 3.3.2 AT SERVICE LIMIT (per ERRATA)
        Lords whose Service marker is in the SAME box as the Levy or
        Campaign marker Disband. They may Muster again later.
        - Place cylinder on Calendar a number of boxes right of the
          current box equal to Service Rating (if Levy), or right of
          the NEXT box (if Campaign, 4.8.2).
        - Set aside Comune cylinder and mat if any.
        - Return Forces and Assets to pools; discard cards at mat.
        - Set aside mat(s), Service marker, remaining Vassal markers.
        - This does NOT trigger Revolt or Treachery (Errata removed
          the "but trigger no Revolt or Treachery" line; the Beyond
          Service Limit Revolt/Treachery rule covers that case).

  NOTE: A Lord who Disbands because his last unit was lost outside
  combat (1.6) also Disbands.

================================================================================
STEP 3.4 - MUSTER
================================================================================

  Guelphs then Ghibellines. Each on-map Lord, in turn, may take Levy
  actions up to his Lordship rating. Complete one Lord before the next.

  REQUIREMENTS to use Lordship:
    - Lord must have begun this Muster segment at a Friendly Locale
      AND be Unbesieged (may be Bypassed).
    - URBAN ARMY exception: a Podesta may use Lordship from his Main
      Seat even if Besieged there.

  EACH LEVY ACTION (= 1 Lordship point) does ONE of:

    [ ] 3.4.1 LEVY OTHER LORDS - Roll Fealty for another Lord:
          - The target Lord must be Ready (cylinder at or left of Levy
            marker) and have a Seat that is Friendly, Unbesieged, and
            (if Ruined) not occupied by Enemy Lords (may be Bypassed or
            Ravaged).
          - Roll 1 die: <= Fealty Rating = success.
          - EXCEPTIONS:
              EXILE ARMY: If a Podesta's Main Seat is Enemy aligned, it
                costs 2 actions (not 1) to roll for him.
              URBAN ARMY: Podesta may Muster at his Main Seat even if
                Besieged (subject to Stronghold Size limit).
              EMERGENCY ARMY: If any Enemy Lord is at a Podesta's Main
                Seat, treat him as Ready regardless of Calendar box.
          - On success: place cylinder at a free Seat, place mat with
            starting Forces/Assets/Vassal markers, place Service marker
            on Calendar (Service Rating boxes right of current).
          - Newly Mustered Lords may NOT themselves take Levy actions
            this segment.

    [ ] 3.4.2 LEVY VASSALS - Muster a Vassal of this Lord:
          - Vassal's Service marker on mat must be face-up (Ready).
          - Vassal's Seat must be Friendly (may be Besieged, Bypassed,
            Ruined, or Ravaged).
          - Slide Service marker into Forces area; add Vassal's Forces
            from the pool.
          - SPECIAL VASSALS EXCEPTION: Carroccio, Sestieri, Terzi have
            NO Vassal Seats. They Muster only at the Leading City via
            Call to Arms (3.5.3). Sestieri/Terzi can be Mustered outside
            Call to Arms ONLY if the Comune mat is already in play with
            those markers Ready AND the Commander is inside (not
            Bypassing/Besieging) his Leading City. Roll die <= number
            shown on Service marker.
          - ALTOPASCIO: starts set aside; the Tau Company Capability
            enables Firenze or Lucca to Muster it for 0 actions (its
            Altopascio Vassal Seat must be Friendly).
          - TAGLIA Capability: lets certain Lords Muster one of each
            other's Unmustered Vassals to their own mats.

    [ ] 3.4.3 LEVY TRANSPORT - 1 action adds 1 Cart to mat. Podesta of
              Pisa (only) may add 1 Ship instead.

    [ ] 3.4.4 LEVY CAPABILITY - 1 action obtains 1 Capability card
              (lower half of an Arts of War card) for the side or for
              this Lord:
              - Card's Coats of Arms show eligible Lords ("Any", "ALL",
                "Not", or specific Coats).
              - "This Lord" Capabilities go at the bottom edge of that
                Lord's mat (max 2 per mat; never two with the same
                title). A Commander may Levy "This Lord" cards to his
                Comune mat (each mat has its own 2-card limit).
              - Other Capabilities tuck at the side's map edge.
              - Levying a Capability blocks that card's Event from
                ever occurring.

================================================================================
STEP 3.5 - CALL TO ARMS (optional, conditional)
================================================================================

  EXCLUDED in Scenario A (Dolenti Note - Preamble rule) and Scenario E
  (Lasciate Ogne Speranza - Exhaustion rule).

  Either side may declare if AT THIS POINT IN PLAY:
    (1) That side's VP score is at least 4 below the other, OR
    (2) That side drew a War Event this Levy (3.1.3), OR
    (3) The Guelphs play Treasurers (F23) and pay 2 Coin.

  If ANY side declares, BOTH sides follow the procedure once (Guelphs
  then Ghibellines). At most one Call to Arms per Levy. Each step is
  optional and may be skipped.

  ORDER WITHIN EACH SIDE:

    [ ] 3.5.1 GATHER - Free March
          - Each Unbesieged Lord MAY March using his Command rating
            (modified as applicable). Commanders may March in a group.
          - May NOT enter any Locale with an Unbesieged/Unbypassed Enemy
            Lord or un-Ruined Enemy Stronghold.
          - Each Lord that does March must end CLOSER to his Leading
            City (Firenze for Guelphs, Siena for Ghibellines).
          - Do NOT mark Moved. Lords do NOT Feed.

    [ ] 3.5.2 COMMANDER TO ARMS
          - If desired, Disband the Commander as if At Service Limit
            (with his Comune if on map).
          - Then, if desired and able per 3.4.1, Muster the Commander
            inside his Leading City from ANY Calendar box (Ready or not).

    [ ] 3.5.3 COMUNE - if Commander is inside his Leading City (even
              Besieged or Bypassed):
          - Set out the Comune mat with its starting Assets and Special
            Vassals (if not already in play).
          - Stack the Comune cylinder beneath the Commander cylinder
            (Besieged: must be room - Stronghold Size limit).
          - Muster Special Vassals to the Comune mat. If the Comune is
            put into play this step, it MUST end up with at least its
            Carroccio (unless Captured) plus one Sestiere or Terzo.
          - All remaining Unmustered Sestieri/Terzi may Muster
            automatically (no roll needed - this is the only auto path).
          - The side may then reassign "This Lord" cards between
            Commander and Comune mats (neither ending with more than 2).

    [ ] 3.5.4 ALLIES - EITHER (not both):
          - Conduct an extra Muster segment with ONE of that side's
            Lords (= his Lordship in Levy actions, per 3.4), OR
          - Muster any one non-Commander Lord automatically (no Fealty
            roll, no Levy action; otherwise per 3.4.1 incl. Urban Army).

================================================================================
CAMPAIGN PHASE (4.0) - STEP-BY-STEP CHECKLIST
================================================================================

================================================================================
STEP A - CAPABILITY DISCARD (pre-Plan)
================================================================================

  Guelphs then Ghibellines. Each side MUST select and discard any
  side-wide Capability cards exceeding their number of Mustered Lords
  (NOT counting "This Lord" Capabilities).
    - Comuni do NOT count as on-map Lords for this purpose.

================================================================================
STEP B - PLAN (4.1)
================================================================================

  Both sides simultaneously build face-down Plan stacks of Command
  cards. Number of cards by Season:
    Spring  = 7
    Summer  = 6
    Autumn  = 7
    Winter  = 4

  [ ] 4.1.1 SELECTING CARDS
        - May use any Command action cards (numeraled) and Pass cards.
        - Treachery cards may be used ONLY if previously added to deck
          via Revolt triggers or Events.
        - Pass cards fill the Plan when a side has too few on-map Lords.

  [ ] 4.1.2 ARRANGING STACKS
        - Stack 4-7 selected cards in chosen order, face down.
        - Top card is the first to flip.
        - Plan order may NOT be rearranged once built.
        - Players may inspect their OWN Plan and the OTHER side's
          already-played cards.

  [ ] 4.1.3 LIEUTENANTS (optional, only during Planning)
        - Place a Lord's cylinder on top of one other Friendly cylinder
          at the same Locale. Upper = Lieutenant, lower = Lower Lord.
        - Lasts the entire Campaign unless one is removed.
        - One Lower Lord max per Lieutenant. The Lower Lord may not be
          a Lieutenant.
        - Neither Commander nor Comune may be a Lieutenant or Lower Lord.
        - Move together in March, Avoid, Retreat, etc.
        - If one Disbands, the survivor becomes a normal Lord.
        - Revealing a Lower Lord's Command card = Pass.
        - May NOT Withdraw into a Castle (Size 1) or enter an Outpost.

================================================================================
STEP C - ACTIVATION (4.2)
================================================================================

  Guelphs then Ghibellines alternate flipping the top card of their
  stack. Continue until BOTH stacks are exhausted.

  Each card flip:

  [ ] REVEAL the top Command card. It is either:

      (1) A COMMAND ACTIONS CARD (with a numeral) - 4.2.1
          The named Lord (if on the map) takes up to that many actions
          from the Command Menu (4.2.2), in any order:
              March, Siege, Storm, Sally, Supply, Forage, Ravage,
              Tax, Pass; Pisa may also Sail.

          ACTION RESTRICTIONS:
            - Besieged Lords (4.3.5) may only Sally, Pass, or
              perhaps Forage (or use Reinforced Walls).
            - Siege, Sail, and Tax each use the ENTIRE card (one action
              effectively spends them all).
            - Starting a Siege (Besiege/Encamp) ends the card's actions
              after resolving Feed/Pay/Disband.
            - Any Battle or Storm ends that card's actions.
            - A Lord may use multiple actions on the same Command type
              (e.g. 3 actions = 3 Forages).

      (2) A TREACHERY CARD (4.2.3)
          The Lord named takes NO normal Command actions, but may
          attempt Revolt (4.7.5) or Bribe (4.7.6), or Pass.

      (3) A PASS CARD, a Lower Lord's card, or a card of a Lord not on
          the map (4.2.4) - nothing happens. Play passes.

  [ ] AT END OF CARD - FEED / PAY / DISBAND (4.8)
      Both sides resolve:
        4.8.1 FEED - For each Lord on either side marked Moved/Fought:
              - 1 Provender or Loot per 1-6 units on mat
              - 2 Provender/Loot per 7-12 units
              - 3 Provender/Loot per 13+ units
              - SHARING: Lords must Feed own Forces first, then Share
                Provender/Loot at the same Locale.
              - UNFED: Service marker shifts 1 box LEFT (Service AND
                Vassal markers if using Advanced Vassal Service).
              - GREED: may not remove Assets beyond this requirement.
        4.8.2 PAY - Guelphs then Ghibellines may Pay (per Levy 3.2).
        4.8.2 DISBAND - All Lords on both sides check Service limit
              and Disband as required (per 3.3.1-.2). Errata: Beyond-
              Service-Limit Revolt/Treachery DOES apply. Podesta cylinder
              goes to NEXT Calendar box +Service Rating boxes.
        4.8.3 Remove Moved/Fought markers from all Lords.

  [ ] CONTINUE: the OTHER side flips its next card.

================================================================================
STEP D - END CAMPAIGN (4.9)
================================================================================

  After both Plan stacks are exhausted, resolve in order:

  [ ] 4.9.1 GROW (only on certain Turns)
        At the end of each late-Spring (April-May) and Autumn
        (October-November) Turn - Calendar boxes 2, 5, 8, 11, 14:
          - Guelph then Ghibelline player each MUST select and reduce
            the ENEMY'S Ravage markers on the map to 1/2 their total,
            rounded UP.
          - Adjust VP accordingly.

  [ ] 4.9.2 RANSOM / LANGUISH
        Guelphs then Ghibellines may pay Coin to recover Captured
        Knights from their Captured Knights box.
          - Cost: 1 Coin total per 6 units captured, rounded UP. Must
            pay the FULL amount (partial Ransom not allowed).
          - Coin goes to the OTHER side, who distributes among its Lords.
          - The paying side selects HALF (rounded UP) of those units
            and distributes to its on-map Lords. Remainder returns to
            the pool.
          - LANGUISH (if no Ransom or unable): the OTHER side rolls on
            its Revolt table OR adds 1 Treachery, one of either per 6
            captured (rounded UP). Revolt rolls in lieu of Treachery
            are mandatory.
          - Lords involved may be Bypassed or Besieged.

  [ ] 4.9.3 GAME END CHECK
        If this was the scenario's last 60-Day Turn, the game ends -
        side with more VP wins (5.3); tie = draw. Otherwise, proceed.

  [ ] 4.9.4 REPAIR (Errata)
        Remove ONE Siege marker from each TOWN and CITY that has 3 or
        4 Siege markers. (Errata: replaced "Stronghold" with "Town and
        City" - Castles do NOT get this Repair.)

  [ ] 4.9.5 WASTE
        Guelphs then Ghibellines must select and discard one Asset OR
        one "This Lord" Capability card from each of their Lords who
        has MORE THAN ONE of any type of Asset or more than one such
        card. Example: 2 Carts + 1 Loot + 1 card = must discard one
        (Cart, Loot, or card - owner's choice).

  [ ] 4.9.6 RESET
        - Set aside all Treachery cards used in either side's Plan
          this Campaign (whether or not used to trigger Revolt/Bribe).
        - Unstack any Lieutenants and Lower Lords.
        - Discard all "This Campaign" Events.
        - Guelphs then Ghibellines may discard any Arts of War cards
          back into their decks (1.9.1).
        - Advance the Campaign marker to the next 60 Days box and flip
          it to Levy.

================================================================================
VICTORY (5.0)
================================================================================

A side wins immediately if at any point during Campaign (4.0) the OTHER
side has NO Mustered Lords on the map (5.2 Campaign Victory - regardless
of VP).

Otherwise victory is determined at Game End (5.3) - whoever has more VP
wins, tied = draw.

VP SOURCES (5.1, tracked on Calendar with round Victory markers):
  + 1 VP    per friendly Allegiance marker on the map
  + 1/2 VP  per friendly Ruins marker on the map
  + 1/2 VP  per friendly Ravaged marker on the map
  + 2 VP    per Captured (enemy) Carroccio held

Adjust VP markers IMMEDIATELY when placing or removing the underlying
markers (2.2.5).

================================================================================
KEY TIMING NOTES & FOOTGUNS
================================================================================

- Capability Discard (start of Campaign) is EASY to forget. Excess
  side-wide Capabilities must go.

- Newly Mustered Lords in 3.4 cannot themselves take Levy actions that
  same segment. They CAN, however, act in Call to Arms 3.5.4 Allies.

- Service shift in Disband Beyond Service Limit (3.3.1) triggers Revolt
  AND Treachery for the enemy - 1x for regular, 3x for Podesta. The
  Errata removed the previous wording that suppressed this.

- Disband At Service Limit during Campaign (4.8.2) puts the Lord on the
  NEXT Calendar box + Service Rating (not the current box). Errata clarifies.

- GROW removes the ENEMY'S Ravage markers, NOT your own. (You choose
  WHICH of theirs to remove - take advantage.)

- Ransom is all-or-nothing per side. You cannot partially Ransom.

- Languish gives the captor a CHOICE between Revolt roll OR Treachery
  card per 6 unransomed prisoners (rounded up). Revolt rolls "in lieu of
  adding Treachery are mandatory" - meaning if you choose Revolt, you
  must roll (not skip).

- Repair (4.9.4 Errata) does NOT remove Siege markers from Castles.

- Reset sets aside ALL Treachery cards in the Plan this Campaign,
  whether used or unused. They must be re-acquired (via new Revolt
  triggers) to be available again.

- Call to Arms can only be declared at the moment between Muster (3.4)
  and Campaign (4.0). If neither side declares then, no CtA this Turn.

- War Event triggers Call-to-Arms eligibility ONLY for the side that
  drew it that Levy. Treasurers (F23) lets the Guelphs declare even
  without a War Event or VP lag.

- The Plan card count is set by the Season the CURRENT Turn is in,
  per the Calendar box color.
