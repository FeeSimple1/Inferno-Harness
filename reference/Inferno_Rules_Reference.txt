PART 1: SEQUENCE OF PLAY & LEVY
================================================================================
 
GAME ROUND STRUCTURE
--------------------
Each 60-Day Turn has two phases:
  1. LEVY     - Prepare armies (3.0)
  2. CAMPAIGN - Command lords on the map (4.0)
 
Guelphs (purple) go first in each step unless noted. Alternate Command card
play: one Guelph, one Ghibelline, etc.
 
Seasons & Plan Size
  Season   Cards in Plan
  Spring        7
  Summer        6
  Autumn        7
  Winter        4
 
 
LEVY PHASE (3.0)
================
 
3.1 Arts of War
---------------
  1. Draw Capabilities (1st Levy only): Each side draws 2 random Arts of
     War as Capabilities (bottom half)
  2. Draw Events (2nd+ Levy): Each side draws & resolves 2 Events (top
     half)
 
Levying a Capability blocks its Event. Hold Events kept secret until played.
 
3.2 Pay
-------
Coin: Remove Coin from any Lord's mat to shift that Lord's (or another Lord
at same Locale) Service marker 1 box right on Calendar.
Loot: Lord must be at Friendly Locale free of Siege (may be Bypassed).
Remove Loot as Coin (1 Loot = 1 Coin, or 2 Loot/Coin for Podesta).
Podesta Lords cost 2 Coin (or Loot) per box shift.
 
3.3 Disband
-----------
Beyond Service Limit: Lords whose Service marker is left of the current
Turn box are removed.
  - Regular Lord: Removed permanently
  - Podesta: Cylinder placed 3 boxes ahead (= Service Rating); may Muster
    again later
 
Revolt & Treachery: When a Lord Disbands (or is removed by combat),
enemy rolls on Revolt table (once for regular, 3x for Podesta) and adds
Treachery cards (1 for regular, 3 for Podesta).
 
3.4 Muster
----------
Lords on the map take Levy actions up to their Lordship rating. Each
action spends 1 point. Complete one Lord before the next.
 
Requirements: Lord must be at Friendly Locale, Unbesieged (Podesta
exception: Urban Army at Main Seat even if Besieged).
 
Urban Army: A Podesta can use Lordship from Main Seat even if Besieged
there and can be Mustered at Main Seat even if Besieged.
 
Emergency Army: If any Enemy Lords are at a Podesta's Main Seat, treat
that Podesta as Ready to be Mustered (wherever his cylinder is on the
Calendar).
 
Levy Actions (each costs 1 Lordship):
  - Levy Other Lords (3.4.1): Roll Fealty to Muster another Lord at his
    Seat
  - Levy Vassals (3.4.2): Muster a Vassal (face-up Service marker,
    Friendly Vassal Seat)
  - Levy Transport (3.4.3): Add 1 Cart (or 1 Ship for Pisa Podesta)
  - Levy Capabilities (3.4.4): Take 1 Capability card from unused Arts of
    War
 
Fealty Roll (Mustering a Lord)
Lord must be Ready, in a Turn box at/left of Levy marker, have a free Seat
(Friendly, not Enemy-occupied, not Ruins). Seat may be Bypassed or
Ravaged.
 
Roll 1 die <= Fealty Rating = Success. Place Lord at Seat, set up mat
with starting Forces & Assets, place Service marker ahead by Service Rating
boxes.
 
Exile Army: If Podesta's Main Seat has Enemy Allegiance, costs 2 Levy
actions to try.
 
3.5 Call to Arms
----------------
Conditions (any one): Lower score by 4+ VP, drew a War Event, or plays
Treasurers Event + 2 Coin.
 
Steps:
  1. Gather (3.5.1): Each Unbesieged Lord may March (Command rating)
     toward Leading City
  2. Commander to Arms (3.5.2): May Disband then re-Muster
     Commander at Leading City
  3. Comune (3.5.3): If Commander is inside Leading City, may
     Muster/augment Comune mat with Carroccio + Sestieri/Terzi
  4. Allies (3.5.4): Extra Muster segment with any one Lord, OR
     auto-Muster any non-Commander Lord
 
Lord Ratings Summary
  Rating      Symbol           Effect
  Fealty      Die pips         Roll to Muster (3.4.1)
  Service     Box numeral      Calendar boxes on Muster/Disband
  Lordship    Shield numeral   Levy actions per turn (3.4)
  Command     Card numeral     Actions per Command card (4.2.1)

PART 2: CAMPAIGN PHASE - Plan, Command, March, and Approach
================================================================================
 
4.1 PLAN
--------
Each side builds a Plan stack of Command cards (seasonal total).
  - Select from Command deck (3 action cards per Lord + 4 Pass cards +
    Treachery cards)
  - Stack in any order, face down. Top card = first Activation
  - May include Treachery cards obtained via events/combat
  - Add Pass cards as needed to fill seasonal total
 
4.1.3 Lieutenants
During Plan only: stack a Lord cylinder on top of another at the same
Locale. Upper Lord is Lieutenant; Lower Lord is led. They move and act
together for the entire Campaign.
 
Neither Commander nor Comune may be Lieutenant or Lower Lord. A
Lieutenant + Lower Lord may not Withdraw into a Castle (Size 1).
 
4.2 COMMAND
-----------
Alternate flipping top Command cards: Guelph, Ghibelline, Guelph...
  - Action card: Lord gets actions = Command Rating
  - Treachery card (4.2.3): No actions; may attempt Revolt (4.7.5) or
    Bribe (4.7.6) or Pass
  - Pass card (4.2.4): No actions, play passes to other side
 
4.2.2 Command Menu (Actions)
  Action    Cost                       Notes
  March     1 (or 2 if Laden)          Move to adjacent Locale (4.3)
  Siege     Entire card                Advance Siege at enemy Stronghold (4.5.1)
  Storm     1 action (ends card)       Attack a Besieged Stronghold (4.5.2)
  Sally     1 action (ends card)       Besieged Lord attacks Besiegers (4.5.3)
  Supply    1 action                   Add Provender from Seats/Routes (4.6)
  Forage    1 action                   Seek food from current Locale (4.7.1)
  Ravage    1 (or 2 at City/Town)      Place Ravaged marker at enemy Locale (4.7.2)
  Sail      Entire card                Pisa only; move by sea (4.7.3)
  Tax       Entire card                Podesta gains 1 Coin (or 2) (4.7.4)
  Pass      1 action                   Do nothing (4.7.7)
 
Besieged Lords may only Sally, possibly Forage, use Reinforced Walls, or
Pass. Battle/Storm ends the card's remaining actions.
 
4.3 MARCH
---------
An Unbesieged Lord spends 1 Command action (2 if Laden) to March to an
adjacent Locale via a connecting Way. Place Moved/Fought marker.
 
Ways: Gray Roads (faster) and brown Tracks (slower). Unladen 1st March
along Road = 0 actions. Laden March along Track costs 2 actions.
 
Laden Status (4.3.2)
  - A Lord with any Loot is always Laden
  - A Lord with more Provender than Carts is Laden (moves max 2x along
    Track, Unladen along Road)
  - Lords with >2 Provender per Cart may not move
 
Unladen: 1st March of a Command card along Road = 0 actions.
 
4.3.4 Approach (Entering Enemy Locale)
When a Marching Lord enters a Locale of an Unbesieged, Unbypassed
enemy Lord, check immediately:
  1. Avoid Battle: Inactive Lords may move away (Unladen only, not
     across Approach Way, not to Locale with Unbesieged enemy). Lords
     Avoiding to an Unbesieged Enemy Stronghold mark as Bypassing it.
  2. Withdraw: Inactive side may Withdraw into a Friendly Stronghold at
     that Locale (up to Size)
  3. Battle: If enemy Lords remain (not in Stronghold), fight immediately
     (4.4)
  4. Besiege or Bypass: If Lord(s) outside an Enemy Stronghold (4.3.5)
 
4.3.5 Besiege or Bypass
  - Besiege: Place Siege marker; skip remaining actions, go to
    Feed/Pay/Disband
  - Bypass: Place Bypass marker; continue actions on this Command card
    except further March (Lord stays at Locale)
 
Bypass allows more actions but skips sieging. Encamp (4.3.6) converts
Bypass to Siege for 1 March action.
 
4.3.6 March from Bypass
  - Depart: Lord at Bypass may March away normally.
  - Encamp: Bypassing Lord may spend 1 March action to replace Bypass
    with Siege marker; skip remaining actions.
  - Sortie: Besieged Friendly Lord inside a Bypassed Stronghold may
    spend 1 March action to Approach the Bypassing enemy (may trigger
    Battle). Only a Commander or Lieutenant can Sortie a group.
 
4.8 FEED / PAY / DISBAND
------------------------
After each Command card, both sides Feed Moved/Fought Lords, then may
Pay, then check Disband.
 
  Units    Provender/Loot Required
  1-6      1
  7-12     2
  13+      3 (max)
 
Underfed: Shift Service marker 1 box left for each Lord that was not fully
Fed.
 
Sharing: Lords at same Locale must share to Feed unfed Friendly Forces
first.

PART 3: BATTLE RULES
================================================================================
 
Field Battle Procedure (4.4)
 
4.4.1 BATTLE ARRAY
------------------
Players Array Lords on the Battle mat or mats:
  - Attackers position first, then Defenders
  - Each side: up to 3 Front positions (Left, Center, Right) + Reserve
  - Active Lord must start at Front Center
  - Attackers fill Front L/R with one other Lord each, if available;
    remainder in Reserve
  - Defender places one Lord directly opposite each Front Attacker, center
    first, then L/R as able
  - Comune (if Commander is Active) may start at Front Center as a
    separate Lord
 
4.4.2 BATTLE ROUNDS
-------------------
Each Round proceeds through these steps:
  1. Concede the Field? Attacker then Defender may declare Concede
     (final round, halved Hits)
  2. Reposition: Advance Un-Routed Reserve Lords into empty Front; fill
     empty Center from L/R
  3. Strike: Each step in order: Def. Archery -> Att. Archery -> Def.
     Horse -> Att. Horse -> Def. Foot -> Att. Foot
  4. Hits & Protection: Assign Hits, roll Protection/Armor
  5. Rout: Units failing Protection are Routed
 
Strike & Flanking
Strike: Front Lords Strike the Lord directly opposite. If none opposite,
the Lord is Flanking: it Flanks the closest Front enemy Lord (L or R of
center; owner chooses).
 
Flanking Hits: Total Hits from Flanking + directly opposed Lords, round
up. A Flanking Lord at owner's option may absorb Hits from Strikes at
other Friendly Lords (if Flanking all Striking enemies).
 
Initiative: Within each Strike step, Striking Lords choose order of Strike,
Lord by Lord. Flanking Lords choose to absorb before opposed Lords
resolve.
 
Total Hits per Unit
  Unit Type                         Piece           Melee Hits   Archery Hits
  Ritter (German Knights)           Silver wedge    2            -
  Cavalieri (Italian Knights)       Green wedge     1            -
  Berrovieri (Constables)           Tan wedge       1/2          -
  Light Horse                       Brown wedge     1/2          1/2
  Men-at-Arms                       Steel bar       1            1/2
  Armigeri (Trained Militia)        Green bar       1/2          1/2 (Crossbow)
  Militia                           Brown bar       1/2          1/2
  Villici (Laborers)                Tan bar         1/2          -
 
Total all Hits for that step including Flanking. Round up. Mixed Archery
with Crossbows: round Hits as Crossbow. No dice - Hits are automatic from
Forces.
 
Assign Hits to Units
Hits apply to Forces of the opposed, Flanked, or Flanking Enemy Lord.
Owner chooses which unit takes each Hit.
 
Exceptions:
  - Select Target: Crossbows (and Sudden Clash event) let the Striking
    side choose which enemy unit takes each Hit
  - Storm Attack: Hits must go to Armored before Unarmored units
 
Protection (Saving Rolls)
Each Hit either Routs a unit or is saved by a Protection roll:
 
  Type                 Roll Range    Notes
  Walls                1-4 (base)    Stronghold defense; cancel one Hit per success
  Armor                varies        Ritter 1-4, Cavalieri 1-3, Men-at-Arms 1-2,
                                     Berrovieri 1-2
  Crossbow vs Armor    Armor -2      Min Armor "1"
  Unarmored            1             Light Horse & Militia avoid Rout on 1 only
  Villici              -             No Protection; removed immediately on Hit
  Siegeworks (Storm)   1-[#Siege]    Attacker's Walls in Storm vs. Archery + Melee
 
A unit may absorb multiple Hits in succession (roll Protection for each).
 
Rout
A unit that fails Protection is Routed - slide below the line on the Lord's
mat. Routed units no longer Strike or absorb Hits.
 
A Lord whose last Unrouted unit Routs is Routed out: remove from Array.
Adjust Flanking immediately.
 
4.4.3 Ending the Battle
-----------------------
A side that Conceded or has no Unrouted Lords loses the Battle.
 
Losing side: All Lords must Retreat to adjacent Locale (no enemy, not
Besieged/Bypassed), Withdraw into Stronghold, or be Removed. Retreat
may join Friendly Lords already Bypassing an Enemy Stronghold.
 
Marching Attackers must Retreat to the Locale they Approached from.
Sallying Attackers Withdraw back into Stronghold. Defenders may not
Retreat along the Approach Way.
 
4.4.4 Losses (Routed Forces)
----------------------------
Roll 1 die per Routed unit vs. Protection (Armor or Unarmored range)
  - Retreated without Conceding / Attacking in Storm: Fail unless roll "1"
  - Within range = unit recovers. Outside range = unit Lost (returned to
    pool)
  - Knights' Quarter: Cavalieri/Ritter removed in Battle (roll 3-6) go to
    Captured Knights box
 
Spoils (4.4.3)
  - Losing Lords who were Removed or Retreated without Conceding
    transfer all Assets (except Ships). If has Carroccio: other side
    captures it (+2 VP).
  - Lords who Conceded & Retreated transfer only excess Loot and
    Provender beyond Laden limit.
  - Lords who Withdrew keep all Assets.
 
 
================================================================================
PART 4: SIEGE, STORM, & SALLY - Stronghold Operations (4.5)
================================================================================
 
4.5.1 SIEGE
-----------
Any Besieging Lord may use his entire Command card to advance the
Siege:
 
Surrender
If no Besieged Lords inside: Roll for Surrender.
  - Roll dice = Stronghold's Value (1, 2, or 3)
  - Each die <= Siege markers + Ravage markers = Accepted
  - If all dice accepted: Stronghold is seized
 
On Surrender:
  - Set Allegiance (place markers = Value); adjust VP
  - Remove all Siege markers
  - No Spoils awarded
  - Revolt & Treachery: Roll Revolt table = Value times; add Treachery
    cards = Value
 
A Ravage marker adds +1 to the Surrender die-roll range (regardless of
Ravage color).
 
Siegeworks
If no Surrender (or Besieged Lords present): add 1 Siege marker (max 4)
if Besieging Lords >= Stronghold's Size.
 
Siege markers erode: at end of each Campaign, remove 1 Siege marker
from each Town/City with 3-4 Siege markers (Repair, 4.9.4).
 
4.5.2 STORM (Sack)
------------------
Any Lord outside a Besieged Stronghold may use 1 Command action to
Storm it. Proceed as Battle (4.4) with these exceptions:
 
Storm Array
  - Attacker: Active Lord (or Comune) in Front; others start in Reserve
  - Defender: Besieged Lords in Front behind Garrison; others in Reserve
  - Each side may Reposition 1 Lord from Reserve to Front per round
    (up to Size)
 
Stronghold Effects in Storm
  - Garrison: Free Foot units per Strongholds table (Melee + Archery).
    Men-at-Arms = x1/2 Hits, Armigeri = x1 Crossbowmen that Select
    Targets with -2 to Armor.
  - Garrison Melee Strikes add to Defending Lords' Strikes (round up)
  - All Defending units Melee Strike before Attacking units (unlike Battle)
  - Siegeworks = Attacker's Walls (value = # of Siege markers against
    Archery + Melee)
  - Attacking Lord must absorb Hits with Armored units first
  - Berrovieri on Attack: Melee x1 (not x1/2)
  - Each Lord of each side adds at most 6 Melee Hits (Archery unlimited)
 
Ending the Storm
Storm ends when # of Rounds completed = # of Siege markers, or if one
side all Routed, or Attacker Concedes.
  - Attackers lose: Return Siege markers to map. Locale remains Besieged.
  - Defenders lose (Sack): Ruin the Stronghold (see below)
  - Routed Defending units always roll Protection. Routed Attacking units
    that fail to roll "1" are removed.
 
Sack (Defenders Lose Storm)
Ruin the Stronghold:
  - Remove all Siege, Allegiance, Walls+1 markers
  - Place Ruins marker (opposite color from printed Allegiance); 1/2 VP
  - Spoils: Loot, Provender, Coin each = Stronghold Value, plus all
    non-Ship Assets & Carroccio of losing Lords inside
  - Knights' Quarter: Garrison Cavalieri (= Size) + all losing
    Cavalieri/Ritter -> Captured Knights
  - Remove each losing Lord per 4.4.5 (Revolt + Treachery)
  - Revolt: Roll = Value times; add Treachery = Value
 
4.5.3 SALLY
-----------
A Besieged Lord may spend 1 Command action to attack Besiegers.
Follow Battle rules (4.4) with exceptions:
  - Array: Besieged = Attacker (Active Lord or Comune in Front),
    Besieger = Defender
  - Reposition mimics Storm (1 Lord per round up to Size)
  - Siegeworks: Defenders (Besiegers) receive Siegeworks as Walls in
    Storm
  - Attackers do NOT receive Walls or Garrison
  - No advance after combat for Besieged
  - Losing Defenders Retreat normally. Losing Attackers (Besieged)
    Withdraw back into Stronghold.
  - Raid: If Sallying Attackers lose, remove all but 1 Siege marker.
 
Relief Sally (4.4.1)
When a side Approaches a Locale that is also Besieged, Besieged Lords
may join the Battle as a Relief Sally:
  - Array Sallying Attackers per Sally (behind Defenders, facing Sallying
    front)
  - Siegeworks benefit only Strikes by Sallying Attackers
  - If Attackers lose, Sallying Lords Withdraw back into Stronghold;
    reduce Siege markers to 1

PART 5: SUPPLY, OTHER COMMANDS, END CAMPAIGN, AND VICTORY
================================================================================
 
4.6 SUPPLY
----------
An Unbesieged Lord may spend 1 Command action to add Provender from
his Seats via Routes.
 
Sources & Routes (4.6.1)
Source: Lord's own Seat(s) (not Vassal Seats), Outpost Seats, or Port
(with Ships). Source must be an Unruined Stronghold or Outpost, not
Besieged or Bypassed by enemy.
 
Route: Unbroken path of Locales & Ways from Lord to Source. Route
blocked by Locales with Enemy Stronghold/Lord - unless Besieged or
Bypassed. Ruins/Ravaged do not block.
 
Transport: Need 1 Cart per Provender per Way crossed along each Route.
 
A Ruined Seat cannot be a Source. But a Ruined Locale does not block
Routes.
 
4.6.2 Add Provender
  - Up to 1 per Size of each Stronghold Seat used (not Ruins)
  - +1 per Outpost Seat
  - +1 per Ship on Pisa's mat (not in Winter) if Source is a Port
  - Max 16 Provender per Lord
 
4.7 OTHER COMMANDS
==================
 
4.7.1 Forage
------------
1 Command action. Locale must not be Ravaged; Lord not Besieged by >=
Stronghold's Size enemy Lords.
  - Friendly Stronghold (not Ruins): Auto +1 Provender
  - Elsewhere (including Ruins): Season matters - Summer: +1 auto;
    Spring/Autumn: roll 1-3 = +1, 4-6 = nothing; Winter: no Forage
    outside Friendly Strongholds
 
4.7.2 Ravage
------------
1 action at Castle; 2 actions at City/Town. Place Ravaged (1/2 VP)
marker in opposite color from original Allegiance. Locale must be
currently Enemy, not yet Ravaged.
 
Loot gained: +1 Provender; at City/Town also +1 Loot. With Berrovieri
(or Gualdana + Horse): 2 Provender if Castle; 2 of each if City/Town.
 
Ravage markers removed at Grow step (4.9.1): halve enemy Ravage
markers at end of each Spring and Autumn Turn.
 
4.7.3 Sail (Pisa Only)
----------------------
Podesta of Pisa uses entire card to move by sea to any Port free of
Unbesieged enemy Lords. Not in Winter. Needs Ships (1 per Horse, 1 per
Provender, 2 per Loot). May act as Lieutenant.
 
4.7.4 Tax
---------
Unbesieged Lord at any Seat (including Outpost Seat, but NOT Vassal Seat)
uses entire card to gain 1 Coin (or 2 Coin if Podesta).
 
4.7.5 Treachery - Revolt
------------------------
On a Treachery card, Unbesieged Lord may attempt Revolt of a currently
Enemy Stronghold (not Ruins/Outpost) at or adjacent, with no enemy Lord
there or adjacent.
 
Procedure: Commit 1-4 Coin. Roll dice = Stronghold Value. If ALL dice <=
Coin committed = Revolt (pay Coin). If any die > Coin = no Revolt (keep
Coin).
 
4.7.6 Treachery - Bribe
-----------------------
On Treachery card, Unbesieged Lord with >=1 Coin may try to Bribe an
enemy Vassal as a Turncoat.
 
Target: Regular Vassal (not Special) that is Mustered at same/adjacent
Locale or Unmustered at same/adjacent Vassal Seat.
 
Roll 1 die: If > Vassal's Service Rating = success (pay 1 Coin, take
Vassal's marker + Forces). If <= = fail (keep Coin).
 
4.9 END CAMPAIGN
================
  1. Grow (4.9.1): At end of each Spring/Autumn Turn, reduce enemy
     Ravage markers to 1/2 (round up); adjust VP
  2. Ransom (4.9.2): Pay Coin (1 per 6 units, rounded up) to recover
     Captured Knights. Languish if declined.
  3. Game End (4.9.3): If final Turn, side with more VP wins
  4. Repair (4.9.4): Remove 1 Siege from each Town/City with 3+ Siege
     markers
  5. Waste (4.9.5): Each Lord discards 1 excess Asset or "This Lord"
     Capability
  6. Reset (4.9.6): Set aside Plan Treachery cards, unstack Lieutenants,
     discard "This Campaign" Events, advance Campaign marker
 
5.0 VICTORY
===========
  Source                                VP
  Each Allegiance marker on map         1 VP
  Each Ruins marker on map              1/2 VP
  Each Ravaged marker on map            1/2 VP
  Captured enemy Carroccio              2 VP
 
Campaign Victory (5.2): If a side has no Mustered Lords on map at any
point, the other side wins immediately.
 
End of Scenario (5.3): At Game End step of last Turn, side with more VP
wins. Tie = draw.

REVOLT & TREACHERY (1.4)
========================
 
Revolt Triggers
---------------
Roll on Revolt table when:
  - A Lord is Disbanded Beyond Service or Removed by combat (once
    for regular; 3x for Podesta)
  - A Stronghold Surrenders or is Sacked (= Value times)
  - Languish of Captured Knights (if Ransom declined)
  - Certain Events specify Revolt rolls
  - Treachery card used for Revolt action (4.7.5)
 
1.4.1 Revolt Eligibility
Only an Enemy Stronghold with no Enemy Lords there or adjacent can
Revolt. Ruins and Outposts never Revolt.
 
1.4.2 Revolt Table Procedure
Roll 1 purple die + 1 gold die, cross-reference on Revolt table:
  - Rebellion: If result names a Locale that is eligible, and a Lord
    cylinder or Allegiance marker of the rolling side is within 1 Locale
    -> Revolt (1.4.4)
  - Submission: If "X" result, select a Stronghold with Enemy Allegiance
    eligible + adjacent to rolling side's Lord -> Revolt
 
1.4.4 Switching Allegiance by Revolt
Revolt adds Allegiance markers = Stronghold Value of the opposite printed
Allegiance (or removes all current markers).
 
Exiles: After any Allegiance switch, the losing side slides its Lord Service
markers 1 box right or cylinders 1 box left per Allegiance marker placed
or removed.
 
1.4.3 Treachery Cards
Each Lord has 1 Treachery Command card (starts set aside). Treachery
cards are added to a side's Command deck by:
  - Revolt triggers (Disband, combat removal, Surrender, Sack, Languish)
  - Certain Events
 
On Campaign, Treachery cards enable Revolt (4.7.5) or Bribe (4.7.6)
instead of normal Command actions. Used Treachery cards are set aside
again at Reset (4.9.6).
 
STRONGHOLDS (1.3.1)
===================
  Type     Size/Value   Notes
  City     3            Largest; hosts many Lords; 3 Allegiance markers
  Town     2            Mid-size; 2 Allegiance markers
  Castle   1            Smallest; 1 Allegiance marker; no Lieutenant
                        pair inside
 
Outposts: Map-edge Locales, only owning Lord may enter. Never Revolt or
get Ruined.
 
VP MARKERS ON MAP
=================
  Marker                         VP        Placed By
  Guelph/Ghibelline Allegiance   1 VP each Revolt, Surrender, Sack
  Ruins                          1/2 VP    Sack (Storm)
  Ravaged                        1/2 VP    Ravage action
  Captured Carroccio             2 VP      Battle/Storm Spoils
 
Ruins and Allegiance markers never occupy the same Locale. Costruttori
Capability repairs Ruins.
