TERMINOLOGY NOTES
-----------------
- Lord ratings: Fealty (F) / Service (S) / Lordship (L) / Command (C)
- Vassals have a Service Rating (used for Bribe defense per 4.7.6 and the
  advanced Vassal Service rule per 3.4.2). Vassals do NOT have Command Ratings.
- "Comune" (one m) = Commander's second mat (Firenze, Siena only).
- "Commune" (two m's) = vassal type (Pistoia Commune, Bologna Commune, etc.).
- Sestieri (Firenze), Terzi (Siena), and Carroccio Special Vassals have NO
  Vassal Seats. They Muster only at the Leading City (3.4.2 EXCEPTION).
- Special Vassals (Sestiere, Terzo, Carroccio, Altopascio) cannot be Bribed
  (4.7.6).
- Podestà Lords: Firenze, Arezzo, Colle, Lucca, Siena, Pisa (1.5.1).
  Special Pay, Revolt, removal, Muster, and Tax effects apply.
- Commanders: Firenze Podestà (Guelph) and Siena Podestà (Ghibelline).
  Each can attach a second "Comune" mat via Call to Arms (3.5.3).

SET-ASIDE SPECIAL VASSAL
=============================================================================
 
ALTOPASCIO (Tau Company)
------------------------
Side:     Guelph (Firenze OR Lucca)
Type:     Special Vassal, set aside at start
Seat:     Altopascio (must be Friendly to Muster)
Forces:   1 Cavalieri
Notes:    Activated only when Tau Company Capability (F22) is in play.
          Tau Company gives Firenze and Lucca Lordship +1 each (every Muster
          and Call to Arms it is in effect, whether or not Altopascio is
          Mustered).
          Either Firenze or Lucca may Muster Altopascio for 0 actions per
          usual Vassal Muster rules (3.4.2).
          Discard of Tau Company Capability Disbands Altopascio.
          Cannot be Bribed (4.7.6 - Special Vassals are immune).
