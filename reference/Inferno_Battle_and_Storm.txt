================================================================================
INFERNO - BATTLE & STORM COMBAT REFERENCE
================================================================================
Source: Inferno Rules of Play (Living Rules 2023-04-10), primarily Sections
        4.4 (Battle), 4.5.2 (Storm). Cross-referenced with Playbook and
        Official Errata (10 April 2023).

When the Rules of Play or Errata conflict with any other source, the Rules of
Play and Errata take precedence (per user instruction).

This reference walks through Battle and Storm combat step by step. For
Approach, Avoid Battle, Withdraw, Besiege vs Bypass, Encamp, Sortie, Sally,
and the Siege action, see the Siege reference. For action costs and what
Storm/Sally cost on a Command card, see the Command Actions reference.

================================================================================
SECTION 1: WHEN COMBAT HAPPENS
================================================================================

BATTLE (4.4) occurs when a Lord Approaches an Enemy Lord (4.3.4-.5) who
does NOT Avoid Battle or Withdraw. ALL Lords in the Locale who are NOT in
a Stronghold must participate.
  - Per the 4.4 Errata, the participation rule is that Lords NOT inside
    a Stronghold (Withdrawn into one) must Battle. This includes
    Bypassing or Besieging Lords already at the Locale (they're outside
    the Stronghold). Only Lords inside a Stronghold (Withdrawn, or
    Besieged inside) sit out.

STORM (4.5.2) occurs when an Unbesieged Lord outside a Besieged Stronghold
uses a Command action to Attack the Stronghold. The Storm rules use Battle
procedure with modifications.

SALLY (4.5.3) occurs when a Besieged Lord uses a Command action to attack
the Besiegers. Sally uses Battle procedure with Storm-like Array/
Reposition.

RELIEF SALLY (4.4.1): When a side Approaches a Locale where it is also
Besieged, Besieged Lords may join the Attack for NO added Command actions.
See Section 4 below.

MARKER: Place the Battle/Storm marker at the combat Locale as a reminder.

================================================================================
SECTION 2: SETUP
================================================================================

2.1 BATTLE ARRAY (4.4.1)
------------------------

Players Array Lords involved on the Battle mat OR with their Lord mats on
the table.

  ORDER OF PLACEMENT:
    [1] Attacker first, then Defender.
    [2] Each side may place a Lord in up to 3 Front positions: LEFT,
        CENTER, RIGHT.
    [3] OTHER Lords start in RESERVE.

  ATTACKER:
    - The ACTIVE Lord MUST start at Front Center.
    - Fill in Front Left and/or Right with other Attacking Lords present
      (one each).
    - Remaining Lords go to Reserve.

  DEFENDER:
    - Must place one Lord directly OPPOSITE each Front Attacking Lord,
      filling Center first, then Left and/or Right as able.
    - Other Defending Lords go to Reserve.

  COMUNE: A Comune (1.5.1) Arrays, Repositions, Flanks, and Routs as if a
  SEPARATE Lord. It may start at Front Center if its Commander is the
  Active Lord. Note: a Commander and his Comune cannot BOTH start at Front
  Center; in Battle the Active Lord starts Center but in Storm the
  Attacker chooses one only - see 2.2 below.

  HIDDEN MATS: If using Hidden Mats Option, reveal mats (only) for combat
  per 1.5.2.

2.2 STORM ARRAY DIFFERENCES (4.5.2 ARRAY)
-----------------------------------------

Storm uses Battle rules (4.4.1-.2) EXCEPT:

  - Each side's Front row begins with AT MOST 1 LORD.
  - Attacker's Front Lord is the ACTIVE Lord (or his Comune, but only
    one - not both at once).
  - Other Lords (and Comune if not chosen) start in RESERVE.

  Stronghold Effects in Storm Setup:
    - Defender receives the Garrison units shown for that Stronghold
      type on the Strongholds table (see Section 8). Garrison is
      SEPARATE from any Defending Lord's units.
    - Defender uses Walls 1-4 (base, plus any Walls+1 marker).
    - Attacker uses SIEGEWORKS = the number of Siege markers in front
      of his Lord(s) as his own Walls.

2.3 SALLY ARRAY DIFFERENCES (4.5.3)
-----------------------------------

Sally uses Battle rules EXCEPT:

  - Each side begins with 1 Lord in Front. Attacker = Sallying Active
    Lord. Defender = a Besieging Lord.
  - Other Lords start in RESERVE.
  - In each Round after the first, Attacker then Defender may add ONE
    Lord from Reserve to Front, up to Stronghold Size.
  - If all Front Lords Routed, a Reserve Lord (if any) MUST move to
    Front.
  - Defenders (the Besiegers) receive SIEGEWORKS = # Siege markers as
    their own Walls.
  - Attackers (the Sallying Besieged) get NO Walls and NO Garrison.
  - A Comune counts as a separate Lord for Array.

================================================================================
SECTION 3: EVENTS
================================================================================

After Array, in order:
  [1] Attacker may play Held Events that specify "in Battle" or "in
      Storm" (as applicable).
  [2] Defender then may play Held Events that specify "in Battle" or
      "in Storm" (as applicable).

(Events drawn fresh during Battle are not standard - Events here are
those Held from prior Levy draws, 3.1.3.)

================================================================================
SECTION 4: RELIEF SALLY (4.4.1)
================================================================================

A special case: when a side Approaches (4.3.4) a Locale where it is ALSO
Besieged inside a Stronghold, the Besieged Lords may join the Attack at no
added Command action cost.

  ARRAY AND REPOSITION:
    - Sallying Attackers Array and Reposition per Sally rules (4.5.3)
      but BEHIND the Defenders (the Approaching side's Enemy).
    - Any Reserve Defenders are Arrayed as if Front Defenders, facing
      the Sallying Attackers.
  STRIKES:
    - Sallying Lords Attack Reserve Defenders or (if none) Front
      Defenders as if Flanking them all equally closely.
  SIEGEWORKS BENEFIT applies to Strikes by Sallying Attackers ONLY.
  LOSS:
    - If the (combined) Attackers lose, Withdraw Sallying Lords back
      into the Stronghold AND reduce Siege markers there to ONE
      (per the Sally Raid rule in 4.5.3).

================================================================================
SECTION 5: BATTLE ROUND STRUCTURE (4.4.2)
================================================================================

After Array (+ Events + any Relief Sally setup), Battle proceeds Round by
Round. Each Round follows this exact sequence:

  (A) CONCEDE THE FIELD?
  (B) REPOSITION (in Round 2 and later only)
  (C) STRIKE
       1. Defending Archery
       2. Attacking Archery
       3. Defending Horse Melee
       4. Attacking Horse Melee
       5. Defending Foot Melee
       6. Attacking Foot Melee
  (D) Check for NEW ROUND or END

Resolve each Strike step FULLY (Hits, Walls roll if any, Protection,
Rout) before moving to the next step.

================================================================================
SECTION 6: CONCEDE THE FIELD (4.4.2)
================================================================================

At the START of each Battle Round, the Attacker then the Defender may
declare that the Battle will END after this Round, with that side as the
loser - but with REDUCED LOSSES.

  - Battles last AT LEAST ONE Round (no Concede in Round 1 is meaningful
    because Concede declarations apply to the CURRENT Round's resolution).
  - Place the PURSUIT marker between the two sides, pointing toward the
    Conceding side.
  - The Conceding side will HALVE its Hits against the Enemy in THIS
    FINAL Round (round up within each Strike step).

STORM CONCEDE DIFFERENCES (4.5.2):
  - In Storm, only the ATTACKER (not the Defender) may Concede.
  - Concede may happen at the start of any Round AFTER the first.
  - On Concede in Storm, the Storm ends immediately. Attackers lose
    (see Storm-specific loss handling in Section 11).

SALLY: follows Battle rules for Concede.

================================================================================
SECTION 7: REPOSITION (4.4.2)
================================================================================

In each Round AFTER the first, Reposition occurs in this order:

  ADVANCE: Attacker then Defender slides Unrouted Lords in Reserve into
    any EMPTY Front positions (one each).

  CENTER: If a Front Center position is empty AFTER Advance, the side
    (Attacker then Defender) MUST select and slide one Lord from Front
    Left or Right to fill its empty Center position.

STORM REPOSITION (4.5.2):
  - Each Round AFTER the first, Attacker then Defender MAY add ONE
    Lord from Reserve to Front - UP TO STRONGHOLD SIZE (Castle = 1
    total, Town = 2 total, City = 3 total).
  - If all Front Lords Routed, a Reserve Lord (if any) MUST move to
    Front.

SALLY REPOSITION (4.5.3): same as Storm.

================================================================================
SECTION 8: STRIKE SEQUENCE (4.4.2)
================================================================================

Each Round's Strike step proceeds through six sub-steps in fixed order
(Battle). At each sub-step, only certain unit types Strike (Foot/Horse,
Archery/Melee).

  BATTLE INITIATIVE ORDER:
    1. Defending ARCHERY
    2. Attacking ARCHERY
    3. Defending HORSE Melee
    4. Attacking HORSE Melee
    5. Defending FOOT Melee
    6. Attacking FOOT Melee

  STORM INITIATIVE ORDER (4.5.2 STRONGHOLD EFFECTS):
    1. Defending Archery
    2. Attacking Archery
    3. ALL Defending Melee (Horse AND Foot)    <-- key difference
    4. ALL Attacking Melee (Horse AND Foot)

  Within each step, the Striking side determines order of Lord-by-Lord
  Strikes. Then any Flanking Lords (see below) choose whether to absorb
  Strikes before opposed Lords. Resolve Hits, Walls rolls (if any),
  Protection rolls, and Rout BEFORE moving on - NOT simultaneously.

  EXAMPLE: Attackers take Hits from Defending Archery (Step 1), resolve
  all Rout, THEN Attacking Archers Strike (Step 2).

================================================================================
SECTION 9: HITS, FLANKING, AND TARGETING (4.4.2)
================================================================================

9.1 HITS GENERATION
-------------------

Hits are NOT rolled - they are automatic based on the Forces Striking.
Each Striking unit causes 1/2, 1, or 2 Hits depending on its type and the
Strike type (Archery or Melee). See Forces table (Section 12) below.

  TOTAL HITS for each step: sum all Hits from all Striking Lords (and
  Flanking Lords against the same target), THEN round up.

  MIXED ARCHERY: When Crossbows and normal Archery combine, round Hits
  IN FAVOR of Crossbows. EXAMPLE (per Errata): A Garrison of 1 Men-at-
  Arms (x1/2 Crossbow) + 1 Militia (x1/2 normal Archery) = 2 Archery
  Hits, of which 1 counts as Crossbows.

9.2 FLANKING
------------

Flanking happens whenever a Lord at Front has NO Enemy Lord directly
opposite him. He Flanks the closest Front Enemy Lord (Center may choose
Left or Right).

  - Total Hits from Flanking + directly opposed Lords against the same
    target, THEN round up.
  - A Flanking Lord may, at owner's option, absorb Hits from Strikes
    directed at other Friendly Lords, PROVIDED he is Flanking all of
    the Striking Enemy Lords.

9.3 PURSUIT (CONCEDE EFFECT)
----------------------------

The Conceding side HALVES its total Hits against the Pursuing side.
Round all fractions UP within each Strike step.

9.4 APPLY HITS TO LORDS
-----------------------

Hits apply to the Forces of the opposed, Flanked, or Flanking Enemy Lord.

A player with a Flanking Lord CHOOSES whether the Flanking or directly
opposed Lord absorbs the Hits.

If a Lord Routs and creates a NEW Flanking situation, apply remaining
Hits accordingly.

9.5 STORM ATTACK TARGETING (4.5.2)
----------------------------------

Hits against the Storm ATTACKER must select ARMORED units before
Unarmored units, regardless of who is choosing what.

(This is automatic for the Defender's Hits against the Attacker, and
also constrains the Attacker if for some reason they direct Hits to
themselves; in practice it means Attackers' Light Horse and Militia
shield their Armored units only after Armored absorb hits.)

================================================================================
SECTION 10: PROTECTION (4.4.2)
================================================================================

10.1 ROLL WALLS (Storm and Sally only)
--------------------------------------

If this is a Storm OR a Sally, the side with Walls/Siegeworks rolls
FIRST, BEFORE assigning Hits to units.

  - Roll dice = total Hits just received in that Strike step.
  - Each roll within the Walls range (typically 1-4) nullifies one Hit.
  - For Siegeworks: each roll <= # Siege markers nullifies one Hit.
  - Hits modified differently (e.g. Crossbows give -2 Armor, +1 to Walls,
    etc.) roll SEPARATELY.

  EXAMPLE: 3 Siege markers = Siegeworks Walls 1-3. Each Hit gets a
  Walls roll; rolls of 1-3 cancel.

  EXAMPLE: A "-1" to Walls would nullify ONE Siege marker (4.5.2). So
  3 Siege markers + "-1" = 1-2 effective.

10.2 ASSIGN HITS TO UNITS
-------------------------

After Walls rolls, the OWNER chooses which unit absorbs each surviving
Hit, Hit by Hit.

  EXCEPTIONS:
    - SELECT TARGET (4.4.2): Crossbow Hits and Sudden Clash Event Hits
      let the STRIKING side choose which Enemy unit takes each such
      Hit. After the owner chooses a Lord (e.g. when Flanking), the
      Striking side Selects Targets; then the owner chooses units for
      any other (non-Select-Target) Hits.
    - STORM ATTACK: Armored units must absorb before Unarmored.

  A unit may absorb multiple Hits IN SUCCESSION, shielding fellow units
  - as long as it succeeds on each Protection roll.

10.3 PROTECTION ROLLS BY HIT
----------------------------

For each Hit a unit absorbs, roll 1 die based on the unit type:

  ARMOR: Unit with Armor Protection rolls 1 die. If <= Armor range
    (e.g. 1-3 for Cavalieri), Hit has NO EFFECT - no Rout.

  CROSSBOWS: Crossbow Hits roll vs Armor reduced by -2, MIN 1.
    Example: Cavalieri (Armor 1-3) hit by Crossbow rolls vs 1-1.

  UNARMORED: Light Horse and Militia roll 1 die. Only a "1" saves them
    from Rout. (Their "Unarmd 1" entry.)

  LABORERS: Villici never roll Protection - they're REMOVED on any Hit.
    (Note: removed Villici don't roll for Loss either - they're gone.)

10.4 ROUT
---------

A non-Villici unit is ROUTED the moment it fails a Protection roll.
Slide the Routed unit to the "Routed" section of the Lord's mat.

  - Routed units do NOT Strike further.
  - Routed units do NOT absorb further Hits.
  - A Lord ROUTS at the moment his LAST Unrouted unit Routs - remove
    him from the Array. A new Flanking situation may immediately result;
    apply remaining Hits accordingly.

================================================================================
SECTION 11: ENDING THE BATTLE / STORM (4.4.3, 4.5.2)
================================================================================

11.1 WHEN TO END
----------------

  BATTLE: If neither side Conceded and at least one Lord on each side
  has yet to Rout, begin a new Round at Concede the Field. Otherwise,
  the Battle ends.

  STORM: A Storm ends once
    - The number of Rounds completed = number of Siege markers there, OR
    - A side has all units there Routed, OR
    - The Attacker Concedes.

  Unless ALL Defenders Routed, Storm Attackers LOSE.

  SALLY: Ends when a side has all its units there Routed (or a side
  Concedes per Battle rules).

11.2 RETREAT, WITHDRAWAL, OR REMOVAL (Battle, 4.4.3)
----------------------------------------------------

The LOSING side's Lords must each (owner chooses for each):

  RETREAT to a single adjacent Locale that has:
    - No Enemy Lords, AND
    - No Enemy Strongholds not already Besieged or Bypassed.

  WITHDRAW into a Friendly Stronghold at the Battle Locale (if Sallying
    or Defending at one). Number of Withdrawing Lords <= Stronghold Size.

  Be REMOVED per 4.4.5.

REQUIREMENTS:
  - Defenders may NOT Retreat along any part of the Way the Attackers
    used to Approach.
  - Marching Attackers must Retreat to the Locale whence they Approached.
  - Sallying Attackers (in Sally) must Withdraw back into their
    Stronghold.
  - Lords may NOT Retreat via Sail.

STORM LOSING (4.5.2):
  - Defender losing: SACK (see Section 13).
  - Attacker losing: Attackers neither Retreat nor give Spoils. The
    Locale REMAINS Besieged. Return Siege markers to the map and go to
    Aftermath.
  - Both sides take Losses (Section 12).

SALLY LOSING (4.5.3):
  - Losing Defenders (Besiegers) Retreat normally; Siege ENDS.
  - Losing Attackers (Sallying Besieged) Withdraw back into their
    Stronghold (NOT Retreat).
  - RAID: If Sallying Attackers lose, REMOVE all but 1 Siege marker at
    the Locale (Siegeworks damage). Siege continues.

11.3 SPOILS (Battle, 4.4.3)
---------------------------

Losing Battle Lords may transfer Assets to the winning Lords. The winning
player distributes received Assets among winning-side Lord mats at the
Locale.

  LOSING LORDS WHO WERE REMOVED (4.4.5) OR WHO RETREATED WITHOUT HAVING
    CONCEDED:
      - Transfer ALL their Assets EXCEPT Ships.
      - If the Lord has a Mustered Carroccio (3.4.2, 3.5.3): the OTHER
        side permanently CAPTURES the Carroccio marker (not its Forces)
        and receives +2 VP.

  LOSING LORDS WHO CONCEDED AND RETREATED:
      - Transfer all Loot and any Provender BEYOND what they could
        carry Unladen.
      - Lose no other Assets, do NOT lose their Carroccio.

  LOSING LORDS WHO WITHDREW into a Stronghold:
      - Keep ALL Assets.

  SHIPS are not Spoils (they show ownership, not location).

  STORM SACK SPOILS (4.5.2 - see Section 13).

11.4 SERVICE SHIFTS (Battle, 4.4.3)
-----------------------------------

The LOSING side rolls 1 die for each of its RETREATED Lords:
  - Roll 1-2: shift Service marker 1 box left
  - Roll 3-4: shift 2 boxes left
  - Roll 5-6: shift 3 boxes left

If using Advanced Vassal Service, each of that Lord's Vassals also
shifts.

EXCEPTION: A Lord who Conceded and Retreated WITH HIS CARROCCIO shifts
just 1 box left (regardless of die roll).

Losing Lords who WITHDREW into a Stronghold do NOT shift Service.

================================================================================
SECTION 12: LOSSES (4.4.4)
================================================================================

After Retreat/Withdraw/Removal, BOTH SIDES determine the fate of Routed
units.

12.1 ROLL FOR EACH ROUTED UNIT
------------------------------

Roll 1 die per Routed unit:

  STANDARD: Compare roll to the unit's INHERENT Protection (Armor or
    Unarmored) from the Forces table, UNMODIFIED by Events or
    Capabilities.
      - Roll within range: unit is no longer Routed. Slide above the
        Routed line.
      - Roll outside range: unit is LOST. Remove to pool.

  HARSH RECOVERY: For Routed units of Lords who:
      (a) Retreated WITHOUT having Conceded (Battle), OR
      (b) Were Attacking in Storm,
    units fail UNLESS they roll a "1".

  VILLICI: Already removed when Hit - do NOT roll for Loss.

12.2 LORD REMOVAL (4.4.5)
-------------------------

If a Lord loses ALL his Forces in Battle or Storm:
  - Remove him per 4.4.5: as if Disbanding Beyond Service (3.3.1),
    INCLUDING Revolt and Treachery for the Enemy.
  - Remove a Commander who loses all Forces from his MAIN mat even if
    his Comune still has some.
  - Comune removal adds NO Revolt or Treachery penalty.

NOTES:
  - Later Disband due to Service shifts from this Battle does NOT count
    as removal by combat (no extra Revolt/Treachery).

12.3 KNIGHTS' QUARTER (4.4.4, 4.5.2)
------------------------------------

CAVALIERI and RITTER units removed in combat may be CAPTURED.

BATTLE Knights' Quarter:
  - Any Cavalieri or Ritter that was Routed AND was REMOVED in Battle
    on a Loss roll of 3-6 goes to the OWNER'S Captured Knights box
    (4.9.2).
  - Example: A winning Lord's Routed Cavalieri rolls "3" - they
    RECOVER (Armor 1-3). They are NOT Captured.
  - Example: Cavalieri of a Lord who Retreated without Conceding
    roll "3" - they needed a "1" to recover. They ARE Captured.

STORM Knights' Quarter (per 4.5.2 SACK):
  - On a SACK, the # of GARRISON Cavalieri for that Stronghold (= its
    Size) ALL become Captured, PLUS all Cavalieri AND Ritter of any
    Lord who lost Defending inside.
  - ATTACKERS in Storm NEVER lose units to Knights' Quarter.

================================================================================
SECTION 13: SACK (Storm Defender Loss, 4.5.2)
================================================================================

When Besieged Defenders LOSE a Storm:

  RUIN THE STRONGHOLD:
    - Remove ALL Siege markers there.
    - Remove any ALLEGIANCE markers there.
    - Remove any WALLS+1 markers there.
    - Place ONE RUINS marker there in OPPOSITE color from the Locale's
      ORIGINAL (printed) Allegiance, regardless of Value or who is
      Sacking it.
    - Adjust VP.

  AWARD SPOILS:
    Besieging Lords receive and distribute among their mats:
      - LOOT count = Stronghold Value (1 / 2 / 3 for Castle/Town/City).
      - PROVENDER count = Stronghold Value.
      - COIN count = Stronghold Value.
      - PLUS ALL non-Ship Assets of the losing Lords inside.
      - PLUS any Carroccio of the losing Lords inside.

  KNIGHTS' QUARTER (per 4.4.4-.5):
    - All Garrison Cavalieri (# = Stronghold Size) go to Besieging
      side's Captured Knights box.
    - PLUS all Cavalieri and Ritter of any Lord who lost Defending
      inside.

  REMOVE LORDS:
    - Each losing Lord removed per 4.4.5 - as if Disbanding Beyond
      Service (incl. Revolt and Treachery for the winners).

  REVOLT AND TREACHERY FROM SACK (separate from Lord removal):
    - Winning side MUST roll on its Revolt table # times = Stronghold
      Value.
    - Winning side adds Treachery cards # = Stronghold Value.

  AFTERMATH: Proceed with general Aftermath (4.4.6).

================================================================================
SECTION 14: STRONGHOLDS TABLE (Storm Defense)
================================================================================

All Strongholds use Walls 1-4 base (improved to 1-5 with Walls+1 marker
placed by Reinforced Walls Capability).

  CASTLE (Size 1)
    Walls: 1-4
    Garrison: 1 Men-at-Arms, 1 Militia, 1 Cavalieri.
    Surrender dice: 1 die.

  TOWN (Size 2)
    Walls: 1-4
    Garrison: 2 Men-at-Arms, 2 Armigeri, 2 Cavalieri.
    Surrender dice: 2 dice.

  CITY (Size 3)
    Walls: 1-4
    Garrison: 3 Men-at-Arms, 3 Armigeri, 3 Cavalieri.
    Surrender dice: 3 dice.

GARRISON FORCES IN STORM (4.5.2):
  - Garrison's Foot units have BOTH Melee AND Archery.
  - Garrison Men-at-Arms: Melee x1, Crossbow Archery x1/2 with Select
    Target and -2 Enemy Armor.
  - Garrison Armigeri: Melee x1/2, Crossbow Archery x1 with Select
    Target and -2 Enemy Armor.
  - Garrison Cavalieri: Melee x1.
  - Garrison Militia: x1/2 Melee, x1/2 normal Archery (Castle Garrison
    has 1 Militia per the Reference info file - confirm from the
    Strongholds table; the Garrisons-given list above is per the
    Inferno_Reference_info.rtf and Playbook; the rule text for Castle
    Garrisons uses Militia, while Town/City use Armigeri).
  - Garrisons add their Strikes to those of the Defending Lord (if any),
    rounding up, but do NOT otherwise affect the Lord's Strikes.
  - Garrisons are SEPARATE from any Defending Lord and IGNORE cards
    affecting that Lord individually. Example: a Besieged Lord's units
    don't get Crossbows from the Garrison's status; they get Crossbows
    only via a Capability card.
  - The Defender MUST assign ALL Hits suffered to GARRISON units until
    they are Routed, after which units of the Front Defending Lord
    (if any) absorb Hits.
  - Whenever Routed AND at end of Storm, return Garrison units to
    the pool. (See also Sack Knights' Quarter, 4.5.2.)
  - The FULL Garrison Defends in EACH Enemy Storm action, regardless
    of what happened in previous Storm actions. (Garrison "respawns"
    between separate Storm actions on the same Stronghold.)

SIEGEWORKS (4.5.2):
  The Besieging side places all Siege markers at the Locale IN FRONT
  of its Attacking Lord(s). Those Lords during the Storm have Walls
  with value EQUAL TO the number of Siege markers, against both Archery
  AND Melee Strikes.
    - 1 Siege marker = Siegeworks 1-1 (i.e. Walls 1-1).
    - 2 Siege markers = Siegeworks 1-2.
    - 3 Siege markers = Siegeworks 1-3.
    - 4 Siege markers = Siegeworks 1-4 (= equal to base Walls).

================================================================================
SECTION 15: AFTERMATH (4.4.6)
================================================================================

After Spoils, Losses, Revolt, and Sack (if any):

  [ ] MOVED/FOUGHT: Mark ALL Attacking and Defending Lords as Fought
      (if not already marked). For Storm: includes Lords who stayed in
      Reserve.

  [ ] EVENTS: Discard all Hold Events used in this Battle or Storm.

  [ ] SIEGE: If the combat created or ended a Siege, adjust Siege,
      Allegiance, Ruins, and/or VP markers.

  [ ] RECOVERY: SKIP any Command actions remaining this card. Go to
      Feed/Pay/Disband (4.8).

KEY RULE: A Battle or Storm blocks any further Command actions on the
current Command card.

================================================================================
SECTION 16: KEY TIMING NOTES & FOOTGUNS
================================================================================

- ARRAY: The Active Lord (Attacker) MUST start Center in Battle. In
  Storm, the Attacker has only 1 Front Lord, who must be the Active
  Lord (or his Comune, choose one).

- COMUNE FLANK: A Comune Arrays and acts as a separate Lord. Its
  Battle position is the player's choice, but in Battle the Commander
  (the Active Lord) must be Front Center; the Comune may go Left or
  Right or in Reserve. In Storm, only ONE of Commander/Comune can be
  the Front Lord.

- STRIKE ORDER differs Battle vs Storm: Battle alternates Horse then
  Foot Melee; Storm: ALL Defending Melee, then ALL Attacking Melee
  (Defender's full Melee before Attacker's full Melee).

- WALLS ROLL only happens in Storm and Sally - not in field Battle.

- SELECT TARGET (Crossbows, Sudden Clash) overrides the owner's choice
  of which unit absorbs which Hit. Pavise Shielmen (Palvasari) +
  Balestrieri lets the side Select Target whenever Crossbows fire (per
  3.4.4 Play Note).

- STORM ATTACKERS must take Hits with Armored units BEFORE Unarmored.
  This is enforced regardless of whose choice is in play. It particularly
  affects "shielding" with Villici - you cannot use Villici/Light
  Horse/Militia to shield Cavalieri in Storm Attack.

- CROSSBOWS reduce Enemy Armor by 2 to a MINIMUM of 1. So Cavalieri
  (1-3) -> 1-1 against Crossbows.

- PURSUIT halves the Conceding side's Hits. The Conceding side is the
  LOSER for purposes of Retreat, Spoils, and Service shifts.

- BATTLE ENDS THE CARD. No further actions after Battle, regardless of
  Command Rating. Same for Storm and Sally.

- LOSS ROLLS are made AFTER the Battle resolution (Retreat/Withdraw/
  Remove). Routed-but-recovered units stay; Routed-and-Lost units go
  to the pool. Lords losing all Forces are removed per 4.4.5.

- KNIGHTS' QUARTER in BATTLE is conditional on a 3-6 Loss roll (i.e.
  the unit fails its recovery AND rolls 3+). Cavalieri of WINNERS and
  Withdraw-ers roll vs 1-3 normal Armor for recovery - so they only
  fail (and Capture) on a 4-6 LOSS roll for the WINNING Lord, no wait:

  Let me re-read 4.4.4: "Roll a die for each Routed unit." For the
  WINNERS and for losers who Withdrew or Conceded+Retreated:
    "compare each unit's roll to its inherent Armor or Unarmored
    Protection." So Cavalieri rolls 1-3 to recover.
  For losers who Retreated WITHOUT Conceding (or Attackers in Storm):
    "fail unless they roll a '1'." So they need a 1.

  KNIGHTS' QUARTER (per the rule text): "Put any Cavalieri or Ritter
  Horse unit that was removed in Battle on a roll of 3-6 into the
  owner's Captured Knights box."
  - If WINNING Cavalieri Routs in Battle and rolls 3: it recovers (1-3
    Armor). Not Captured.
  - If RETREATING-Without-Conceding Cavalieri Routs and rolls 3: fails
    recovery (needed 1). Roll was 3-6 -> Captured.
  - If WINNING Cavalieri rolls 4: fails recovery (4 > 3). Roll was 3-6
    -> Captured.
  - If RETREATING-WITHOUT-CONCEDING Cavalieri rolls 1: recovers.

  So Knights' Quarter triggers ONLY if (a) recovery fails AND (b) the
  fail-roll was a 3, 4, 5, or 6. On a roll of 2 by a Cavalieri who
  needed a 1, the unit fails recovery but is LOST to the pool (not
  Captured) - because the roll wasn't 3+.

- IN STORM, defending Cavalieri/Ritter ALL go to the captor's Captured
  box on a Sack (not a 3-6 roll - just all Captured). Attackers
  NEVER get Captured.

- IF an Enemy Lord is REMOVED in combat, the enemy gets Revolt rolls
  AND Treachery cards (per 4.4.5 calling back to 3.3.1). Comune removal
  is exempt.

- A CARROCCIO captured permanently grants +2 VP. If a Lord with
  Carroccio Concedes and Retreats, he KEEPS the Carroccio. If he is
  Removed or Retreats Without Conceding, the other side captures it.

- A Lord BEHIND another Friendly Lord ("Reserve") in Battle does NOT
  Strike but is also not directly Hit unless Flanking results put him
  in line. He CAN be Hit if all Front Lords Routed and he Repositions.

- The Pursuit marker is a physical reminder. Place it pointing at the
  Conceder so you remember which side halves Hits this Round.

- ARCHERY HITS go to both sides each Round (Defender first, then
  Attacker) BEFORE any Melee. A Lord can be Routed by Archery before
  he ever Strikes.

- SACK's Spoils include 2 Coin (for a Town) on TOP of the Sacked
  Lord's Assets and Carroccio. The Stronghold's "natural" 2 Loot, 2
  Provender, 2 Coin (for a Town) are baseline payout.

- After Sack, REMOVED Lords (4.4.5) generate Revolt/Treachery for the
  Sacking side. The Sack itself ALSO generates Revolt/Treachery (=
  Value). So a 3-VP City Sack with one Lord inside can yield Revolt
  rolls / Treachery cards from BOTH triggers.
