================================================================================
INFERNO - SIEGE REFERENCE (Approach, Bypass, Siege, Sally)
================================================================================
Source: Inferno Rules of Play (Living Rules 2023-04-10), primarily Sections
        4.3.4 (Approach), 4.3.5-.6 (Besiege/Bypass/Encamp/Depart/Sortie),
        4.5 (Siege/Storm/Sally), and cross-references to 1.3.1 (Strongholds),
        1.4 (Revolt and Treachery), 4.9.4 (Repair). Errata applied.

When the Rules of Play or Errata conflict with any other source, the Rules of
Play and Errata take precedence (per user instruction).

This reference covers the SIEGE SUBSYSTEM at the strategic level: how Lords
arrive at Strongholds, choose between Besieging and Bypassing, escalate to
Storm, conduct or face Sally, and how Strongholds change hands or rebel as
a result. For combat resolution inside a Battle, Storm, or Sally, see the
Battle & Storm Combat reference. For action costs on a Command card, see
the Command Actions reference.

================================================================================
SECTION 1: STRONGHOLDS - QUICK REFERENCE (1.3.1)
================================================================================

  TYPE     SIZE/VALUE  ALLEGIANCE MARKERS  LORDS MAX  LIEUTENANT/WITHDRAW
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Castle   1           1 marker            1          NO Lt/Lower pair
                                                       inside; max 1 Lord
                                                       Withdraws
  Town     2           2 markers           2          Lt/Lower pair OK
  City     3           3 markers           3          Lt/Lower pair OK

OUTPOSTS (1.3.1): Map-edge purple-arrow Locales. Only the owning Lord
may enter. Never Revolt. Never get Ruined. Can be RAVAGED adjacent (with
La Cavallata).

VP MARKERS at Locales:
  - Allegiance (1 VP each)  - placed by Revolt, Surrender, Sack-removal
                              of Enemy Allegiance.
  - Ruins (1/2 VP)          - placed by Sack (Storm victory), opposite
                              color from PRINTED Allegiance.
  - Ravaged (1/2 VP)        - placed by Ravage action, opposite color
                              from PRINTED Allegiance.
  - Captured Carroccio (2 VP) - on board if captured.

NOTES:
  - Ruins and Allegiance markers NEVER occupy the same Locale.
  - Costruttori Capability repairs Ruins.
  - Reinforced Walls Capability places a side's Walls+1 marker on a
    Seat Stronghold, improving Walls in Storm until Sacked.

SECTION 2: MARCH ARRIVAL - APPROACH (4.3.4)
================================================================================

When a Marching Lord enters the Locale of an Unbesieged, Unbypassed Enemy
Lord, check immediately for Avoid Battle, Withdrawal, Battle, and Siege.

  STEP 1 - AVOID BATTLE (Inactive Lords' option):
    Some or all Inactive Lords may move to one or more adjacent
    Locales. RESTRICTIONS:
      - May NOT Avoid across any part of the Way the Active Enemy Lord
        used to Approach.
      - May NOT Avoid into a Locale with another Unbesieged Enemy Lord.
      - Mark Lords Avoiding into an Unbesieged Enemy Stronghold as
        BYPASSING it.
      - Lords may only Avoid Battle UNLADEN.
      - Lords may discard Loot/Provender to become Unladen and thereby
        Avoid. DISCARDED Assets go to the Approaching Lords as Spoils
        (distributed by Active player).
    Mark Avoiding Lords as Moved/Fought.

    Notes:
      - Cannot Avoid via Sail.
      - The Ambush Event (F1) can prevent ONE Enemy Lord from Avoiding.

  STEP 2 - WITHDRAW (Inactive Lords' option):
    The Inactive side may Withdraw some or all Lords into a Friendly
    Stronghold there (NOT Ruins).
      - Max Lords moving in <= Stronghold Size (1, 2, or 3).
      - Withdrawal alone does NOT mark Moved/Fought.

  STEP 3 - BATTLE:
    If any Enemy Lords there did NOT Avoid AND did NOT Withdraw, BATTLE
    resolves immediately. Marching side is the Attacker (4.4 - see
    Battle & Storm Combat reference).

  After Battle (or if all Enemy Lords Avoided/Withdrew), the Active
  Lord(s) may be outside an Enemy Stronghold - go to Besiege or Bypass
  (4.3.5).

================================================================================
SECTION 3: BESIEGE or BYPASS (4.3.5)
================================================================================

Whenever a side has Lord(s) in a Locale OUTSIDE an Enemy Stronghold
(NOT Ruins) that is NOT yet Bypassed or Besieged - AND if any Enemy
Lords are there, they have all Withdrawn inside - the outside Lord(s)
MUST immediately choose ONE:

  BESIEGE - Place 1 Siege marker on the Stronghold/Lord(s) there.
            SKIP any remaining actions on this card. Go to Feed/Pay/
            Disband (4.8).

  BYPASS  - Place a Bypass marker on the side's Lord(s) outside the
            Stronghold. CONTINUE any actions on this card without
            leaving that Locale. The Bypass marker stays until removed.

KEY: All Lords outside one Enemy Stronghold are EITHER all Besieging
OR all Bypassing, never mixed.

ARRIVING LATER (per Errata, 4.3.5 paragraph 2):
  Lords arriving outside an already Besieged or Bypassed Enemy
  Stronghold join in the existing Siege or Bypass, OR may March on.

REMOVAL OF SIEGE/BYPASS MARKERS:
  Whenever a Besieged or Bypassed Stronghold becomes free of Enemy
  Lords in the Locale, REMOVE all Siege or Bypass markers there.

NOTES:
  - Ruins and Friendly Strongholds do NOT impede March; Lords NEVER
    Besiege or Bypass them.
  - Bypass enables remaining card actions (Supply, Forage, Ravage, etc.)
    in the Stronghold's Locale.
  - Bypass also allows Retreat to or Supply past an Enemy Stronghold.

================================================================================
SECTION 4: BYPASS-STATE COMMAND ACTIONS (4.3.6)
================================================================================

A Lord (or group) BEGINNING a Command card at a Bypass marker - either
Bypassing or Bypassed - has special March options:

  DEPART (4.3.6):
    March to an adjacent Locale NORMALLY (per 4.3.3 costs).
    - Mark Moved.
    - If no Lord remains at the Bypassed Enemy Stronghold, remove any
      Bypass markers.

  ENCAMP (4.3.5):
    Use ONE March action (regardless of Laden status) to:
    - Replace ALL Bypass markers at the Bypassed Stronghold with ONE
      Siege marker.
    - SKIP any remaining actions on this card.
    - Go to Feed/Pay/Disband (4.8).
    - Mark the Encamping Lord(s) Moved/Fought (but NOT other Lords
      there of either side).

  SORTIE (4.3.5 SORTIE):
    Use ONE March action (regardless of Laden status) for a Lord or
    group INSIDE a Bypassed FRIENDLY Stronghold to APPROACH (4.3.4)
    the Bypassing Enemy outside, INSTEAD of moving adjacent.
    - If the Sortieing side loses Battle, Withdraw or Retreat normally.
    - Only a Commander or Lieutenant can Sortie a GROUP (otherwise
      single Lord only).
    - The Bypassing Enemy is now Approached - Battle ensues unless
      they Avoid or Withdraw (which most can't given their Bypassing
      state in the open).

PLAY NOTE: With enough actions, a Lord can Bypass a Stronghold, then
Forage or Ravage, then Encamp on the SAME Command card.

SECTION 5: SIEGE ACTION (4.5.1)
================================================================================

A Besieging Lord may use his ENTIRE Command card to advance the Siege.

PROCEDURE:

  STEP 1 - SURRENDER ROLL (only if no Besieged Lords inside)
  -----------------------------------------------------------
  If the Siege Locale has NO Besieged Enemy Lords inside (only its
  Garrison defends it), the Besieging side may roll for Surrender
  BEFORE adding any Siege marker.

  Roll dice = Stronghold's Value (1 for Castle, 2 for Town, 3 for City).

  If EACH die <= (Siege markers + Ravage markers at the Locale):
    The Stronghold SURRENDERS.

  Max possible total = 4 Siege markers + 1 Ravage marker = 5.

    OUTCOME:
      - Set Stronghold to Besieger's Allegiance: place Allegiance
        markers = Value (or remove existing Enemy Allegiance markers
        already there).
      - Adjust Victory (5.1).
      - REMOVE all Siege markers at the Locale.
      - TERMS: Award NO Spoils. No Sack.

    REVOLT AND TREACHERY (after Surrender):
      - Besieging side rolls on its Revolt table # times = Stronghold
        Value (1.4.2).
      - Besieging side adds Treachery cards # = Stronghold Value
        (1.4.3).

  NOTE: A Ravage marker at the Siege Locale ADDS 1 to the Surrender
  die-roll range REGARDLESS of whose Ravage marker it is. (So one of
  your own Ravage markers there also helps.)

  STEP 2 - SIEGEWORKS (if no Surrender)
  --------------------------------------
  If the Stronghold did not Surrender (either dice failed, or there
  was a Besieged Lord inside, or the Besieger declined to roll):

  IF the Besieging side has AT LEAST as many Lords at the Locale as
  the Stronghold's Size (1/2/3), ADD ONE Siege marker.

  MAXIMUM 4 Siege markers at any one Locale.

  IF the Besieging side has FEWER Lords than Stronghold Size, do
  NOT add a Siege marker. (But the Siege action still consumes the
  card and Feeds Lords.)

  STEP 3 - MOVED/FOUGHT
  ---------------------
  Mark ALL Lords of BOTH sides at the Locale as Fought.

NOTES:
  - Cannot Surrender if there are Besieged Enemy Lords inside (they
    are the defenders).
  - "Starving out" a Besieged Lord: Each Siege action Feeds both
    sides' Lords there. If the Besieged army runs out of Provender/
    Loot, their Service shifts left. If shifted Beyond Service, they
    Disband - then the next Siege action can attempt Surrender.

================================================================================
SECTION 6: STORM ACTION (4.5.2)
================================================================================

A Lord OUTSIDE a Besieged Stronghold may use ONE Command action to
launch an Attack. The Storm uses Battle rules with modifications.

  See the Battle & Storm Combat reference for full procedure.

  KEY STRATEGIC FACTORS:
    - Storm lasts at most # Rounds = # Siege markers there.
    - Defender uses Walls 1-4 (base) + any Walls+1 marker.
    - Attacker uses Siegeworks = # Siege markers as their own Walls.
    - Larger Strongholds have larger Garrisons (1/2/3 of each unit
      type at Castle/Town/City).
    - Only the Attacker may Concede (after Round 1).
    - Each Lord caps at 6 Melee Hits per Round.

  STORM ENDS THE CARD: Like any Battle, Storm blocks any further
  Command actions on the current card.

================================================================================
SECTION 7: SALLY ACTION (4.5.3)
================================================================================

A Besieged Lord may use ONE Command action to Attack the Besiegers
in a Battle.

  See the Battle & Storm Combat reference for full Sally combat.

  KEY STRATEGIC FACTORS:
    - 1 Lord per side at Front initially; 1 may advance per Round
      (cap = Stronghold Size).
    - Defenders (Besiegers, now defending against Sally) receive
      SIEGEWORKS = # Siege markers as Walls.
    - Attackers (Sallying Besieged) get NO Walls and NO Garrison.
    - Losing Defenders Retreat normally; Siege ENDS.
    - Losing Attackers Withdraw back into the Stronghold (NOT
      Retreat). RAID effect: REMOVE all but 1 Siege marker at the
      Locale (Siegeworks damage). Siege continues.

================================================================================
SECTION 8: RELIEF SALLY (4.4.1)
================================================================================

When a side Approaches (4.3.4) a Locale where it is ALSO Besieged
inside a Stronghold, its Besieged Lords may join the Approaching
attack for NO added Command actions.

  See the Battle & Storm Combat reference for full Relief Sally
  combat (Section 4 of that reference).

  KEY STRATEGIC POINTS:
    - Sallying Attackers Array behind the Defenders.
    - Reserve Defenders are Arrayed as if Front Defenders facing the
      Sallying Attackers.
    - Sallying Lords Flank all Striking Enemy Lords equally closely.
    - Siegeworks benefits apply to Strikes by Sallying Attackers ONLY.
    - If combined Attackers lose, Sallying Lords Withdraw back into
      the Stronghold AND Siege markers there are reduced to ONE.

================================================================================
SECTION 9: REVOLT & TREACHERY FROM SIEGE OUTCOMES
================================================================================

The Siege subsystem feeds the Revolt system (1.4) in multiple ways:

  SURRENDER (4.5.1):
    - Roll on Revolt table # = Stronghold Value.
    - Add Treachery cards # = Stronghold Value.

  SACK (4.5.2):
    - For each Lord REMOVED in Sack (4.4.5):
        - Roll on Revolt table 1x (regular Lord) or 3x (Podesta).
        - Add Treachery 1 card (regular) or 3 cards (Podesta).
    - For the Sack itself:
        - Roll on Revolt table # = Stronghold Value.
        - Add Treachery cards # = Stronghold Value.

  LORD DISBAND/REMOVAL BY COMBAT during a Siege Battle/Storm/Sally:
    - Per 3.3.1 / 4.4.5, the Enemy rolls Revolt (1x reg, 3x Podesta)
      and adds Treachery (1 card reg, 3 Podesta). Comune removal:
      no Revolt/Treachery.

  EXILES (1.4.4): Any Allegiance shift on the map shifts the losing
  side's cylinders or Service markers on the Calendar by 1 box per
  Allegiance marker placed/removed.

  REVOLT ELIGIBILITY (1.4.1): An Enemy Stronghold with NO Enemy Lords
  THERE OR ADJACENT can Revolt. Ruins and Outposts never Revolt.

SECTION 10: SIEGE EROSION - REPAIR (4.9.4 per ERRATA)
================================================================================

At the end of EACH Campaign, REMOVE one Siege marker from each TOWN
and CITY that has 3 or 4 Siege markers.

  - Errata changed "Stronghold" to "Town and City."
  - CASTLES do NOT erode their Siege markers via Repair.
  - Towns/Cities with 1 or 2 Siege markers do NOT erode.

This is in the End Campaign sequence:
  4.9.1 Grow
  4.9.2 Ransom/Languish
  4.9.3 Game End check
  4.9.4 Repair          <-- HERE
  4.9.5 Waste
  4.9.6 Reset

PLAY NOTE: Maintain pressure with at least one Lord at the Siege so
the Siege does not lift between Campaigns; if at 3-4 Siege markers,
plan for the erosion.

SECTION 11: SACK VS SURRENDER (Strategic Tradeoffs)
================================================================================

                        SURRENDER (Terms)      SACK (Storm victory)
  -----------------------------------------------------------------
  Trigger              Siege action, no       Defender loses Storm
                       Enemy Lord inside,
                       dice <= Siege +
                       Ravage markers
  Stronghold State     Allegiance flipped     RUINED (until Costruttori)
                       to Besieger's side
  VP Impact            +1 VP per Allegiance   +1/2 VP from Ruins
                       marker (= Value)       (vs. -1 VP per removed
                                              Enemy Allegiance marker)
  Spoils               NONE (Terms)           Loot + Provender + Coin
                                              all = Stronghold Value;
                                              PLUS all non-Ship Assets
                                              of inside Lords; PLUS
                                              any Carroccio.
  Inside Lords         Released (safe         Removed per 4.4.5 (with
                       parole - keep all      full Revolt/Treachery
                       Assets)                effects). Cavalieri/Ritter
                                              Captured.
  Garrison             Returned to pool       Cavalieri Captured;
                                              others to pool.
  Revolt/Treachery     # = Value (once each)  # = Value (once each)
                                              PLUS per-Lord-removed
  Stronghold Future    Yours to garrison      Ruined until repaired
                       and lose to Revolt     (Costruttori)
                       again

KEY TAKEAWAYS:
  - Surrender CONVERTS the Stronghold (+VP via Allegiance).
  - Sack DESTROYS the Stronghold (smaller VP, but big Asset payout).
  - Sacking a rebel Town in your OWN territory places a Ruins marker
    in the OPPOSITE color of PRINTED Allegiance (i.e. ENEMY color).
    This means you LOSE 1/2 VP from that Ruins marker, even though
    you removed Enemy Allegiance markers. Net VP can be NEGATIVE.
    Surrender is better in such cases.

SECTION 12: BESIEGE EXAMPLE WALKTHROUGH (Conceptual)
================================================================================

Lord A marches to an Enemy Castle (Size 1) at Locale X with no
defenders inside.

  TURN 1, Lord A's card:
    1. March (1 action) into X.
    2. Approach (4.3.4): no Enemy Lord = no Avoid/Withdraw/Battle.
    3. Besiege or Bypass (4.3.5):
       - Besiege: place 1 Siege marker. Card ENDS. Feed.
       - Bypass: place Bypass marker. Continue actions.

  TURN 1, later Lord A's card (assume Besieged):
    1. Siege action (whole card):
       - Surrender roll (no Lord inside): 1 die <= 1 Siege marker = 1.
         If roll is 1 -> Castle Surrenders! Place 1 Guelph/Ghibelline
         Allegiance marker. Roll Revolt 1x and add 1 Treachery.
       - If Surrender fails (roll 2-6): IF Lord A is alone (1 Lord =
         Castle Size 1, qualifies for Siegeworks), add a 2nd Siege
         marker.
       - Feed both sides' Lords there.

  TURN 1, Lord A's third card (assume 2 Siege markers, no Surrender):
    1. Siege action again:
       - Surrender roll: 1 die <= 2. Roll 1 or 2 -> Surrender.
       - Otherwise add 3rd Siege marker.

  TURN 1, Lord A's fourth card (4 Siege markers maxed):
    1. Siege action:
       - Surrender roll: 1 die <= 4. Roll 1-4 -> Surrender (67% odds).
       - Otherwise: nothing happens this card (already at max Siege).
       OR
    1. Storm:
       - 1 action launches Storm.
       - Walls 1-4 vs Siegeworks 1-4. Up to 4 Rounds.
       - Garrison = 1 Men-at-Arms + 1 Militia + 1 Cavalieri.
       - On Defender total Rout: SACK. 1 Loot + 1 Provender + 1 Coin
         to Lord A; Ruins marker placed; 1 Revolt roll + 1 Treachery.

Add Ravage of the same Locale (2 actions for Town/City, 1 for Castle)
to boost the Surrender odds by +1 to the die range.

SECTION 13: KEY TIMING NOTES & FOOTGUNS
================================================================================

- BESIEGE-OR-BYPASS choice is MANDATORY: a side cannot just sit in
  an Enemy Stronghold's Locale doing nothing. Lords either Besiege
  (and end card) or Bypass (and may continue).

- BESIEGE ends the card immediately. So plan multi-action cards
  (March 2 + Forage 1) BEFORE Besieging, or Bypass instead.

- BYPASS preserves card actions. Subsequent Lords arriving outside
  the same Stronghold join the Bypass (4.3.5 Errata p17).

- ENCAMP converts a Bypass to a Siege in mid-Campaign. Useful when
  you decide late that you want to lock down a Stronghold.

- DEPART vs ENCAMP: Both happen from a Bypass state, but Depart
  moves away and removes Bypass; Encamp stays and converts to Siege.

- SORTIE is the WAY to break a Bypass without waiting for the next
  Levy. A Lord inside a Bypassed Friendly Stronghold uses 1 March
  action to Approach (and thus initiate Battle with) the Bypassing
  Enemy outside. The Bypassing Enemy CANNOT Avoid (they're already
  in the open at this Locale). They can Withdraw (back into ... wait,
  they have no Stronghold there - they're outside an Enemy Stronghold,
  so they cannot Withdraw). So they Battle or are removed.

- The SURRENDER ROLL is the BESIEGER'S option - they may decline
  it. (Useful if they're stacking Siege markers and don't want to
  risk a low-Value early Surrender; though usually any Surrender is
  good.)

- BESIEGED LORDS BLOCK SURRENDER. You must remove them (via Storm
  Sack, starvation Disband, etc.) before a Surrender roll is possible.

- The "SIEGEWORKS" condition (# Lords there >= Size) means a SINGLE
  Lord cannot add Siege markers to a Town (Size 2) or City (Size 3).
  You need 2 Lords at a Town to add markers, 3 at a City. You CAN
  still Siege-action with fewer (it just doesn't add a marker; it
  Feeds both sides).

- MAX 4 SIEGE MARKERS at any one Locale.

- REPAIR (4.9.4) erodes Siege markers at Towns/Cities with 3-4 markers
  - NOT at Castles, NOT at 1-2-marker Towns/Cities. This sets a
  "natural cap" of effort needed to maintain a serious Siege.

- RAVAGE for SURRENDER: a Ravage marker (any color) at the Siege
  Locale adds +1 to the Surrender die range. A 2-action Town Ravage
  effectively buys +1 to your 2-die Surrender roll on subsequent
  Sieges. Per ERRATA: this is regardless of WHOSE Ravage marker.

- WALLS+1 from Reinforced Walls Capability is permanent until Sack
  removes it (4.5.2). Storm of a Walls+1 Stronghold gives Defender
  base 1-5 Walls in Walls rolls.

- RUINS reset on a Sack. Place 1 Ruins marker in opposite color of
  PRINTED Allegiance. Existing Allegiance markers, Siege markers,
  and Walls+1 markers are REMOVED.

- RUINS PRESERVE SEATS. A Ruined Stronghold still has its Lord/Vassal
  Seats for purposes of Muster (3.4-3.5). But a Ruined Seat cannot
  be a Supply Source.

- A RUINED Stronghold CANNOT be Besieged, Stormed, or Withdrawn into.
  Lords may freely March through. Costruttori Capability rebuilds.

- OUTPOSTS: only the owning Lord may enter. Cannot Revolt. Cannot be
  Ruined. CAN be RAVAGED adjacent (only with La Cavallata).

- SACK + REMOVED LORDS = double-dip on Revolt/Treachery. Each removed
  Lord gives Revolt rolls and Treachery cards per 4.4.5 (1x regular,
  3x Podesta). The Sack itself ALSO gives Revolt/Treachery = Value.
  A City Sack with a Podesta inside = 3 (Sack) + 3 (Lord removal) =
  6 Revolt rolls and 6 Treachery cards selected.

- COMMANDER+COMUNE INSIDE: counts as 1 Lord cylinder for Withdrawal
  purposes but 2 for Array (4.4.1) and Storm Front limits. Comune
  removal in combat does NOT trigger Revolt/Treachery (4.4.5 / 1.5.1).

- SIEGE markers placed BY the Besieger - their color matches the
  Besieger's side (purple for Guelphs, gold for Ghibellines).
  Distinguish from RAVAGE markers (also colored, but opposite of
  printed Allegiance).

- ON SAIL TO BESIEGE: Pisa's Sail (4.7.3) to an Unbesieged Enemy
  Stronghold places a Siege marker on arrival. Cannot Sail to
  Approach Enemy Lords (no Battle by Sail).

- RELIEF SALLY is a powerful 0-action trick: if you Approach with a
  field army AND have Lords trapped inside the Stronghold, those
  Besieged Lords get to join the field Battle (Flanking from the
  rear). Siegeworks help only the Sallying side.

- LOSING ATTACKERS in Storm don't Retreat. They stay outside,
  Besieged Locale unchanged. They just take Losses (with the harsh
  "1-only" recovery for Routed units).
