================================================================================
INFERNO - COMMAND ACTIONS REFERENCE
================================================================================
Source: Inferno Rules of Play (Living Rules 2023-04-10), primarily Sections
        4.2 (Command), 4.3 (March), 4.5 (Siege/Storm/Sally), 4.6 (Supply),
        4.7 (Other Commands), 4.8 (Feed/Pay/Disband). Cross-referenced with
        Playbook and Official Errata (10 April 2023).

When the Rules of Play or Errata conflict with any other source, the Rules of
Play and Errata take precedence (per user instruction).

This reference covers what each Command action does, what it costs, what its
requirements are, and how it interacts with state. For COMBAT resolution see
the Battle & Storm reference; for the Siege subsystem (Approach, Bypass,
Surrender) see the Siege reference.

================================================================================
GENERAL ACTION RULES (4.2)
================================================================================

When a Command actions card flips up, the Lord shown may execute a series
of actions, in ANY order, totaling up to his Command Rating (the numeral
on the card, cylinder, and mat, possibly modified by Arts of War cards).

The Command Menu (4.2.2):
  MARCH      SIEGE      STORM      SALLY      SUPPLY
  FORAGE     RAVAGE     TAX        PASS       SAIL (Pisa only)
  TREACHERY-REVOLT or TREACHERY-BRIBE (only via Treachery card, 4.2.3)

EXCEPTIONS / RESTRICTIONS:
  - BESIEGED LORDS may only Sally, Pass, or perhaps Forage. They may
    also benefit from Reinforced Walls Capability play. They cannot
    March, Supply, Ravage, Tax, Sail, etc.
  - SIEGE, SAIL, and TAX each take an ENTIRE card (effectively use all
    actions on the card).
  - Starting a SIEGE (Besieging a Stronghold by entering its Locale, or
    Encamping from Bypass) ENDS the card's actions after the Siege step.
  - Any BATTLE or STORM ends that card's actions (4.4.6 Recovery).
  - A Lord may use multiple actions on the SAME Command type (e.g. 3
    actions = 3 Forages).

After actions, both sides resolve Feed/Pay/Disband (4.8) on Moved/Fought
Lords. Remove Moved/Fought markers. Other side flips next card.

NOTE: Lieutenant + Lower Lord always move together (4.1.3). A revealed
Lower Lord card = Pass.

================================================================================
MARCH (4.3)
================================================================================

  WHO:        Any Unbesieged Lord (alone or as a group with his
              Commander/Lieutenant).
  COST:       1 action per Locale moved (modified by Laden status and
              Road usage; see below).
  EFFECT:     Move the Lord to an adjacent Locale across one Way.
              Mark Moved/Fought.

CARTS NOT REQUIRED to March. Carts haul Provender; they determine how
much Provender a Lord can take. Coin and Loot do not need Carts.

  4.3.1 GROUP MARCH
    Any/all of a side's Unbesieged Lords at the same Locale as the
    Commander may March with him.
    A Lieutenant's Lower Lord MUST move with the Lieutenant.

  4.3.2 LADEN STATUS
    A Lord is LADEN if any of:
      - He has any Loot on any Way, OR
      - He has more Provender than Carts but not more than twice as
        much, on a Track (or, with Loot, on a Road).
    A Lord with up to 2x Provender per Cart along ROAD only (no Loot)
    is UNLADEN.
    A Lord with MORE than 2x Provender per Cart may NOT move at all
    unless he discards the excess (1.7.2 Greed).

    SHARING: Lords moving as a group share Carts. Count total Provender
    and Carts across all moving Lords to determine Laden status.

  4.3.3 MARCH SPEED
    Standard: 1 action per Way to an adjacent Locale.
    EXCEPTIONS:
      - UNLADEN, the FIRST March of a Command card costs ZERO actions
        if it goes Unladen along ROAD (not Track, not Trackway).
      - LADEN (with Loot, or 2x Provender on Track) costs 2 actions
        instead of 1 per Way.
    TRACKWAY: If a Way uses both gray Road and brown Track, it counts
    as Track (the slower).

  4.3.4 APPROACH (entering a Locale with an Enemy Lord)
    When a Marching Lord enters an Unbesieged, Unbypassed Enemy Lord's
    Locale, check the following choices for Inactive (defending) Lords:

      AVOID BATTLE - Move adjacent. Restrictions:
        - May NOT use the Way the Active Lord just used.
        - May NOT enter a Locale with another Unbesieged Enemy Lord.
        - To Avoid into an Unbesieged Enemy Stronghold's Locale, mark
          the Avoiding Lord as Bypassing it.
        - Only UNLADEN Lords may Avoid. May discard Loot/Provender to
          become Unladen (those discarded Assets go to the Approaching
          Lords as Spoils, distributed).
        - May NOT Avoid via Sail.
        - Mark Avoiding Lords as Moved/Fought.

      WITHDRAW - Move into a Friendly Stronghold there (not Ruins).
        - Number of Lords moving in <= Stronghold Size.
        - Withdrawal alone does NOT mark Moved/Fought.
        - (Same option exists after losing a Battle - see Battle ref.)

      BATTLE - If any Enemy Lords do not Avoid/Withdraw, Battle
              resolves immediately (see Battle reference). The
              Approaching side is the Attacker.

  4.3.5 BESIEGE or BYPASS
    Whenever a side has Lord(s) in a Locale outside an Enemy Stronghold
    (not Ruins) that is not yet Bypassed or Besieged - AND if any Enemy
    Lords are there, all have Withdrawn inside - the outside Lord(s)
    MUST immediately choose one:

      BESIEGE - Place 1 Siege marker. SKIP remaining actions on this
                card. Go to Feed/Pay/Disband.
      BYPASS  - Place a Bypass marker on the side's Lord(s). May
                CONTINUE remaining actions on this card (e.g. Supply,
                Forage, Ravage) without leaving that Locale.

    Lords arriving outside an already Besieged/Bypassed Enemy Stronghold
    may either join the existing Siege/Bypass or March on (Errata, p.17).

    Whenever a Besieged/Bypassed Stronghold becomes free of all Enemy
    Lords in the Locale, remove all Siege OR Bypass markers there.

    KEY: All Lords outside one Enemy Stronghold are EITHER all Besieging
    OR all Bypassing - never mixed.

    PLAY NOTE: Bypass preserves remaining actions. Bypass enables a
    Retreat path or Supply Route past the Stronghold (4.4.3, 4.6.1).

  4.3.6 MARCH FROM BYPASS
    Mark Marching Lords as Moved.

      DEPART  - A Lord/group beginning a Command card at a Bypass marker
                (either Bypassing or Bypassed) may March to an adjacent
                Locale normally. If no Lord remains at the Bypassed
                Enemy Stronghold, remove any Bypass markers.
      ENCAMP  - A Bypassing Lord may use 1 March action (regardless of
                Laden) to replace all Bypass markers there with 1 Siege
                marker, skip the rest of the card, and go to Feed/Pay/
                Disband. Mark Encamping Lord Moved/Fought (not other
                Lords there of either side).
      SORTIE  - A Lord/group inside a Bypassed Friendly Stronghold may
                use 1 March action (regardless of Laden) to Approach
                (4.3.4) the Bypassing Enemy. If they lose Battle, they
                Withdraw or Retreat normally. NOTE: Only a Commander or
                Lieutenant can Sortie a group.

================================================================================
SIEGE (4.5.1)
================================================================================

  WHO:        Any Besieging Lord.
  COST:       ENTIRE Command card (uses up all actions on that card).
  EFFECT:     Advance the Siege. Potentially Surrender the Stronghold
              and/or add a Siegeworks marker.

  See Siege reference for full procedure. Quick summary:

  STEP 1 - SURRENDER ROLL (only if no Besieged Lords inside):
    Roll dice equal to Stronghold Value (Castle = 1, Town = 2, City = 3).
    If EACH die <= total Siege + Ravage markers there (max 4 Siege + 1
    Ravage), the Stronghold Surrenders: set to Besieger's Allegiance
    (place markers = Value, or remove existing Enemy markers), remove
    all Siege markers, no Spoils (Terms).

    REVOLT & TREACHERY follow Surrender:
      - Roll on Revolt table # times = Stronghold Value.
      - Add Treachery cards # = Stronghold Value.

  STEP 2 - SIEGEWORKS (if no Surrender):
    If the Besieging side has at least as many Lords there as the
    Stronghold's Size, add 1 Siege marker (max 4 at the Locale).

  ALL Lords there (both sides) are marked Fought.

  ERRATA NOTE: A Ravaged Locale adds +1 to the Surrender die-roll range
  regardless of WHOSE Ravage marker it is.

================================================================================
STORM (4.5.2)
================================================================================

  WHO:        Any Lord outside a Besieged Stronghold (or a Comune).
  COST:       1 Command action.
  EFFECT:     Launch an Attack on the Stronghold. Resolves as Battle
              with Storm-specific modifications.

  See Battle & Storm reference for full combat procedure.

  ENDS THE CARD: Like any Battle, Storm blocks any further Command
  actions on the current card (4.4.6 Recovery).

  KEY STORM DIFFERENCES FROM BATTLE:
    - Front begins with at most 1 Lord per side.
    - Only the Attacker can Concede (after Round 1).
    - At most 1 Lord per side may Reposition forward per Round (cap
      = Stronghold Size).
    - Defender uses Walls 1-4 (base) + any Walls+1 marker.
    - Attacker uses Siegeworks = # Siege markers as their own Walls.
    - All Defending units Melee Strike BEFORE any Attacking units
      (unlike Battle's Horse-then-Foot Initiative).
    - Attacker absorbs Hits with ARMORED units before Unarmored.
    - Berrovieri Attackers Strike Melee at x1 (not x1/2).
    - Each Lord caps at 6 Melee Hits per Round (Archery uncapped).
    - Storm lasts at most # Rounds = # Siege markers there (or earlier
      if Attacker Concedes or a side has all units Routed).
    - On Defender loss: SACK (see Battle ref).
    - On Attacker loss: Attackers neither Retreat nor give Spoils;
      Locale remains Besieged.
    - Losses: Routed Attacking units recover only on a "1" roll.
              Routed Defending units roll vs their normal Protection.
    - Knights' Quarter: Defending Cavalieri/Ritter from Sacked
      Stronghold all become Captured (not 3-6 roll); Attackers never
      do.
    - All Lords at the Storm Locale (including Reserve) are marked
      Moved/Fought.

================================================================================
SALLY (4.5.3)
================================================================================

  WHO:        Any Besieged Lord.
  COST:       1 Command action.
  EFFECT:     Attack the Besiegers in a Battle.

  See Battle & Storm reference for full combat procedure.

  KEY SALLY DIFFERENCES FROM BATTLE:
    - Array begins with 1 Lord per side at Front (Attacker = Active
      Lord); others Reserve.
    - Reposition: 1 Lord per side may advance per Round, up to
      Stronghold Size.
    - Defenders (the Besiegers, now Defending the Siege lines) receive
      Siegeworks (= # Siege markers, treated as Walls).
    - Attackers (the Sallying Besieged) do NOT receive Walls or
      Garrison.
    - A Comune counts as a separate Lord for Array.
    - On Defender loss: Defenders Retreat normally; Siege ENDS.
    - On Attacker loss: Sallying Attackers must Withdraw back into
      their Stronghold (not Retreat). RAID effect: remove all but 1
      Siege marker at the Locale (Siegeworks damaged by sortie). Siege
      continues.

================================================================================
SUPPLY (4.6)
================================================================================

  WHO:        Unbesieged Lord (Stores & Well Water Capability allows
              Supply while Besieged).
  COST:       1 Command action per Supply.
  EFFECT:     Add Provender to the Lord's mat from a Source.

  4.6.1 SOURCES & ROUTES
    A SOURCE is the Active Lord's OWN SEAT (not Vassal Seats) at an
    Unruined Stronghold or Outpost. If the Active Lord is Pisa's
    Podesta (or sharing with him) using Ships, a PORT may be the Source.

    ROUTE: Unbroken chain of Locales and Ways from the Active Lord back
    to the Source, served by Transport.
      - May NOT include any Locale with an Enemy Stronghold or Enemy
        Lord UNLESS that Stronghold/Lord is Besieged or Bypassed.
      - Ruins and Ravaged Locales do NOT block Supply Routes (but a
        Ruined Seat cannot be a Source).

    TRANSPORT: Active Lord must have or Share (1.5.2) AT LEAST 1 CART
    per Provender, for EACH intervening Way crossed. EXAMPLE: 2 Ways
    away from Source, wanting 3 Provender = 6 Carts needed (2 per
    Provender along Route).
      - At his own Seat or using Ships at a Port: no Carts needed.

  4.6.2 ADD PROVENDER
    The Active Lord adds Provender, within Transport limit:
      - Up to 1 per Size of each Stronghold Seat used (not Ruins) -
        i.e. Castle=1, Town=2, City=3.
      - 1 per Outpost Seat.
      - 1 per Ship on Pisa's mat (only Pisa, and only if Source is a
        Port, and not in Winter).
    Max 16 Provender on a mat (Waste, 1.7.3).

    NOTE: Pisa Sharing Ships works only at a Port Source for that
    specific action.

================================================================================
FORAGE (4.7.1)
================================================================================

  WHO:        Any Lord whose Locale is not Ravaged.
  COST:       1 Command action.
  EFFECT:     Attempt to add 1 Provender to the Lord's mat.

  RESTRICTIONS:
    - Locale may NOT be Ravaged.
    - The Lord may NOT be Besieged by Enemy Lords equal to or more than
      the Stronghold's Size.

  RESOLUTION BY LOCATION AND SEASON:
    - At Unbesieged Friendly Stronghold (not Ruins): AUTOMATIC +1
      Provender, any season.
    - Elsewhere (including Ruins):
        Summer  - AUTOMATIC +1 Provender.
        Spring  - Roll 1 die: 1-3 = +1 Provender; 4-6 = nothing.
        Autumn  - Roll 1 die: 1-3 = +1 Provender; 4-6 = nothing.
        Winter  - NO Forage outside Friendly Strongholds.

  NOTES:
    - Ravage at a Locale blocks Forage there.
    - Forage requires no Sources or Transport.
    - A Lord may Forage even if Bypassed.

================================================================================
RAVAGE (4.7.2)
================================================================================

  WHO:        Unbesieged Lord (may be Bypassing).
  COST:       1 action at a CASTLE; 2 actions at a TOWN or CITY.
  EFFECT:     Place a Ravaged 1/2 VP marker. Gain Provender (and Loot
              at Town/City).

  REQUIREMENTS:
    - Locale must be Enemy aligned (current Allegiance).
    - Locale must not already be Ravaged.

  PLACE RAVAGED MARKER:
    Marker color = OPPOSITE of Locale's ORIGINAL (printed) Allegiance.
    +1/2 VP to the side whose color the marker is.

  GAINS to Active Lord's mat:
    Castle:        +1 Provender. (If Berrovieri present, or Gualdana
                   card + any Horse: +2 Provender.)
    Town or City:  +1 Provender +1 Loot. (Berrovieri/Gualdana: +2
                   Provender +2 Loot.)

  PLAY NOTES:
    - May Ravage even while Besieging.
    - May Ravage Ruins.
    - The La Cavallata Capability extends Ravage to adjacent Outposts.
    - Ravage on an Enemy-Allegiance Stronghold encourages Surrender
      (adds to Surrender die roll). Costs your side 1/2 VP since the
      Ravage marker placed is in the OPPOSITE color of printed
      Allegiance.
    - Ravage markers reduce by half each Grow Turn (4.9.1).

================================================================================
TAX (4.7.4)
================================================================================

  WHO:        Any Unbesieged Lord at one of his Seats (Main, Vassal-as-
              Seat, or Outpost). NOTE: Just a Vassal Seat for a Vassal
              does not count - must be the Lord's own Seat.
  COST:       ENTIRE Command card.
  EFFECT:     +1 Coin to the Lord's mat. PODESTA: +2 Coin instead.

================================================================================
SAIL (4.7.3) - Pisa only
================================================================================

  WHO:        Pisa's Podesta only, at a Friendly Unbesieged Port.
  WHEN:       Any Season EXCEPT Winter.
  COST:       ENTIRE Command card.
  EFFECT:     Move directly to any other Port that is free of Unbesieged
              Enemy Lords.

  REQUIREMENTS BEFORE MOVING:
    - At least 1 Ship per Horse unit moving.
    - At least 1 Ship per Provender moving.
    - At least 2 Ships per Loot moving.
    - Any units, Provender, Loot, etc. that cannot be transported must
      be discarded or left behind (per Greed, 1.7.2).

  SPECIAL:
    - May Sail as a Lieutenant carrying a Lower Lord (4.1.3).
    - If Sailing to an Unbesieged Enemy Stronghold: place a Siege marker
      (Besiege).
    - Mark Sailing Lords Moved/Fought.
    - May NOT Sail to Approach Enemy Lords (cannot start a Battle by
      Sailing).
    - May NOT use Sail to Avoid Battle or Retreat.

  PORT LIST: Includes Strongholds up the Arno River (Firenze, San
  Miniato, etc.) as marked on the map.

================================================================================
TREACHERY - REVOLT (4.7.5)
================================================================================

  WHO:        Active Lord on a flipped TREACHERY card, Unbesieged.
  COST:       The Treachery card itself (Lord takes no other actions).
  EFFECT:     Attempt to flip a nearby Enemy Stronghold to your side.

  TARGET REQUIREMENTS (1.4.1 - Eligible for Revolt):
    - An Enemy Stronghold (not Ruins, not Outposts).
    - At or adjacent to the Active Lord.
    - With NO Enemy Lord there or adjacent.

  PROCEDURE:
    1. Active Lord OPENLY commits 1 to 4 Coin (may Share with Lords at
       same Locale, 1.5.2).
    2. Roll dice equal to target Stronghold's Value (1, 2, or 3).
    3. If EACH die <= number of Coin committed: ACCEPTED.
       - Pay that Coin to the pool.
       - The Stronghold Revolts (1.4.4): add Allegiance markers = Value
         (or remove existing Enemy Allegiance markers if already
         present).
       - Adjust VP.
       - EXILES: Losing side shifts cylinders left or Service markers
         right on the Calendar by 1 box per Allegiance marker placed
         or removed.
    4. If any die > Coin committed: DECLINED. Keep the Coin. No effect.

  NO SPOILS, no further Revolt/Treachery rolls (Terms, 1.4.4).

================================================================================
TREACHERY - BRIBE (4.7.6)
================================================================================

  WHO:        Active Lord on a flipped TREACHERY card, Unbesieged, with
              at least 1 Coin (may Share).
  COST:       The Treachery card itself. 1 Coin if successful.
  EFFECT:     Take an Enemy Vassal as a Turncoat to your mat.

  TARGET REQUIREMENTS:
    The target must be a REGULAR VASSAL (NOT Carroccio, Sestiere,
    Terzo, or Altopascio Special Vassals - they cannot be Bribed).

    AND EITHER:
      (a) Mustered (including as a Turncoat already) with an Enemy
          Lord at the same Locale as OR adjacent to the Active Lord, OR
      (b) Unmustered (not on a mat, and per Advanced Vassal Service,
          not on the Calendar) with a Vassal Seat at the same Locale
          as OR adjacent to the Active Lord.

    Clarification: Vassals of REMOVED Lords (3.3.1, 4.4.5, 6.0) count
    as Unmustered Vassals for Bribe purposes.

  PROCEDURE:
    1. Designate target. Roll 1 die.
    2. If roll > Vassal's Service Rating: SUCCESS.
       - Pay 1 Coin to pool.
       - Move Vassal's Service marker from wherever it is to the Active
         Lord's mat (or his Comune mat).
       - Move Vassal's Forces from the Enemy Lord's mat (to the extent
         surviving) to the Active Lord's mat.
       - If this removes the Enemy Lord's last Forces, that Lord
         Disbands (per 1.6).
    3. If roll <= Vassal's Service Rating: NO EFFECT. Keep the Coin.

  HIDDEN MATS: If using Hidden Mats Option and the designated target
  turns out invalid (wrong Muster state), the Active Lord may designate
  other potential targets until one is found.

  TURNCOATS:
    - A Bribed Vassal is a Turncoat. Bribe may target a Turncoat (to
      flip them back).
    - Turncoats ignore Advanced Vassal Service Disband.
    - When a Lord with a Turncoat Disbands or is removed, his Turncoats
      immediately become AVAILABLE to their ORIGINAL side. If their
      original Lord is on the map, put markers face-up Ready on his mat.

================================================================================
PASS (4.7.7)
================================================================================

  WHO:        Active Lord.
  COST:       Any number of actions (or all of them).
  EFFECT:     Do nothing.

  Also: revealing a PASS card, a LOWER LORD's card, or a Lord NOT on
  the map results in nothing happening for that card (4.2.4).

================================================================================
END-OF-CARD: FEED / PAY / DISBAND (4.8)
================================================================================

After EVERY Command card (both sides resolve):

  4.8.1 FEED
    Every Lord on EITHER side marked Moved/Fought must Feed his Forces:
      - 1 Provender or Loot for  1-6  units on mat
      - 2 Provender or Loot for  7-12 units
      - 3 Provender or Loot for  13+  units (Feed x3, mainly Firenze
        Comune)

    SHARING (1.5.2): Each Lord Feeds his OWN Forces first. THEN, Lords
    at the same Locale MUST Share Provender/Loot to Feed any other
    Friendly Lord at that Locale who could not fully Feed. A side may
    NOT withhold - it must use all available Assets to meet needs.

    UNFED: Any Lord that received LESS than required shifts Service 1
    box LEFT. If using Advanced Vassal Service, his Vassals' markers
    shift too. A Lord needing 2 or 3 Provender but with access to fewer
    still consumes those AND suffers the shift.

    GREED: May NOT remove Assets beyond this requirement.

  4.8.2 PAY (optional)
    Guelphs then Ghibellines may Pay per Levy 3.2 (Coin or Loot at
    Friendly unbesieged Locale).

  4.8.2 DISBAND
    All Lords on both sides check Service limit:
      - BEYOND limit -> Disband Beyond Service (3.3.1) with Revolt/
        Treachery for the Enemy. Podesta cylinder to NEXT box + Service
        Rating (Errata clarifies).
      - AT limit -> Disband At Service Limit (3.3.2). No Revolt.

  4.8.3 REMOVE Moved/Fought markers from all Lords.

================================================================================
INTERACTION NOTES & FOOTGUNS
================================================================================

- MARCH and ENCAMP cost 1 action each but end the card differently:
  March-then-Besiege ends the card. March-then-Bypass does not. Encamp
  from Bypass status ends the card.

- A Lord BEGINNING a card at a Bypass marker may DEPART (move adjacent
  via March) instead of staying. He need not Encamp.

- BYPASS is the only way to use March-card actions for non-March
  Commands (Supply, Forage, Ravage) at a Locale with an Enemy
  Stronghold.

- SUPPLY transport math: 1 Cart per Provender PER WAY between Active
  Lord and Source. Add up all Carts needed across the Route. A Lord at
  his own Seat needs zero Carts.

- A Bypass at an Enemy Stronghold lets a Supply Route pass through that
  Locale (but does not turn it into a Source).

- FORAGE has THREE tracks (Friendly Stronghold = auto, Summer = auto,
  Spring/Autumn = roll, Winter = no go outside Strongholds).

- RAVAGE always places its marker in the OPPOSITE color of printed
  (original) Allegiance, regardless of current Allegiance markers.
  Ravaging a Ghibelline-printed Locale that has flipped to Guelph
  Allegiance still places a GUELPH Ravage marker. The Guelphs get the
  1/2 VP, BUT the marker damages their own economy if they re-take it.

- SAIL is the ONLY Command requiring Ship math (1 per Horse, 1 per
  Provender, 2 per Loot).

- TAX requires a Seat. Vassal Seats only count if they're ALSO this
  Lord's own Seat. A Lord with no Seats at his current Locale cannot
  Tax there.

- A Treachery card flip is BINARY: either Revolt or Bribe (or Pass).
  Cannot do both. The Lord shown on the Treachery card is the Active
  Lord for that action.

- TREACHERY-REVOLT can be DECLINED (no Coin spent if dice fail).
  TREACHERY-BRIBE costs 1 Coin only on success; failure costs nothing
  but the card.

- BRIBE does NOT require sight of the Enemy Lord's mat - only adjacency
  to the Vassal's Lord or to the Vassal's Seat. Hidden Mats works
  around mis-targeting (rule 4.7.6).

- BESIEGED Lords are extremely limited: Sally, Pass, or Forage (within
  the not-fully-Besieged rule for Forage), plus Reinforced Walls play.
  No Supply (unless Stores & Well Water), no March, no Tax, no Ravage,
  no Sail, no Treachery.

- Newly-Bypassed Lords with leftover actions on the same card may use
  them - this is the entire point of Bypass over Besiege. They count
  as "at" the Enemy Stronghold's Locale for adjacency.

- FEED happens after each card regardless of action used, but ONLY for
  Lords marked Moved/Fought. Pure Supply/Forage/Ravage/Tax with no
  movement does NOT mark a Lord Moved/Fought - so no Feed required.

- Siege action marks BOTH sides' Lords at the Locale as Moved/Fought.
  So Besieger AND Besieged Feed after a Siege action.

- After a SACK, the Defender pays Spoils to the Attacker. After a
  SURRENDER (Terms), no Spoils.

- All non-action card flips (Pass, Lower Lord, Lord not on map) yield
  nothing, but the OTHER side still flips next.
